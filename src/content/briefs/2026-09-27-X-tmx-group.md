## What happened (news + tape)

On **September 24, 2026**, CFO **David Arnold** spoke at **CIBC's Eastern Institutional Investor Conference**. Coverage of the remarks circulated on **September 26–27**.

- Arnold said TMX is **"well on track"** toward its **TM2X** objective: grow revenue from **C$1 billion to C$2 billion** in half the time it took to grow from C$500 million to C$1 billion.
- **Aspirational mix targets:** recurring revenue about **two-thirds** of the pie, roughly **half** of revenue outside Canada, and **Global Solutions, Insights and Analytics near 50%**. Recurring is about **53%** today — and management says it will not grow that mix by starving transactional venues.
- **Capital formation:** more IPOs in the first half of 2026 than in all of 2025; Canadian capital raising **up about 15%** year over year through August; financings **up nearly 90%**.
- **Deal slate:** **Cboe Australia closed** and rebranded as **TMX Australia Exchange**; **Refi closed** into VettaFi; **Cboe Canada** still pending Competition Bureau and OSC review; **BOX + MEMX** still needs SEC approval.
- Tape (TSX screener): **+19.8% over three months**, roughly **flat over one year (+1.5%)**, **+370% over ten years**.

## Why it matters

The old story: Canada's exchange utility. The new story: a global market-infrastructure and data platform — listings, trading, clearing, Trayport, Datalinx and VettaFi — with a stated path from C$1B to C$2B in revenue. Growth investors care whether the deal slate and recurring-mix targets stick. Income investors get a modest yield while management funds technology and M&A.

## Bull case

1. **TM2X on track**, with capital-markets activity already strong in 2026.
2. **Closed deals** in Australia and Refi into VettaFi expand data and international reach.
3. **Street targets around C$65–C$71** imply upside if the mix shift lands.

## Bear case

1. **~25x trailing earnings** is a premium to this peer set — progress is partly priced in.
2. **Cboe Canada and BOX + MEMX** still need regulators; timing and dis-synergies are real risks.
3. **Recurring revenue is still about 53%**; transactional volumes can swing with markets.

## What to watch next

1. Competition Bureau and OSC decisions on Cboe Canada.
2. SEC approval on BOX + MEMX.
3. TMX third-quarter results (Yahoo lists October 26, 2026).
4. Whether recurring and international revenue mix keep climbing toward the aspirational targets.
