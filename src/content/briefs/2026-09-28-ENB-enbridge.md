## What happened (news + tape)

In **early September 2026**, Enbridge stacked four catalysts into one news cycle:

- **September 8:** CEO **Greg Ebel** will retire on **December 31, 2026**; **Michele Harradence** becomes President & CEO on **January 1, 2027**.
- **September 9:** Acquire **Tallgrass Energy's** crude transportation business for about **US$2.55 billion** (majority stake in the **Pony Express** pipeline into Cushing) plus **Salt Creek Midstream** Delaware Basin gathering for about **US$600 million**. Management says the deals are accretive to distributable cash flow per share in year one.
- **September 9–14:** A bought-deal common equity offering of about **C$2.6 billion** closed at **C$3.0 billion** including the over-allotment, priced near **C$66.85** a share.
- **Street:** National Bank upgraded to **Strong Buy** with a **C$81** target; BMO upgraded to **Outperform** at **C$79.50**; Raymond James kept a **Hold** at **C$80**. Consensus from 23 analysts sits near **C$79.83**.
- Tape (TSX screener): **−15.9% over three months**, roughly **flat over one year (−2.7%)**, indicated yield near **5.8%**.

## Why it matters

The old story: a steady Canadian utility-style pipeline with a growing dividend. The new story: a larger North American crude franchise — Mainline, Line 5, Pony Express, Permian gathering — funded partly with fresh equity, under a new CEO in 2027. Income investors care whether 5.8% is fair compensation for dilution and leverage. Growth investors care whether Tallgrass and Salt Creek are accretive fast enough to justify the raise.

## Bull case

1. **National Bank and BMO upgraded** after the pullback, with Street targets clustered near **C$80**.
2. Management says the **Tallgrass and Salt Creek** deals are **accretive to DCF per share** in year one.
3. A **5.8% indicated yield** after a **~16% three-month slide** is rare for this franchise if leverage returns inside the band.

## Bear case

1. **C$3 billion of new equity** dilutes holders right as leverage printed above the target ceiling (~**5.1x** Debt/EBITDA).
2. Trailing **price to free cash flow near 62x** — GAAP FCF and payout optics are messy.
3. **CEO succession** plus Line 5 and regulatory risk can keep a discount on the multiple even if the deals close cleanly.

## What to watch next

1. Close timing and integration for Tallgrass and Salt Creek.
2. Whether Debt/EBITDA moves back inside **4.5–5x**.
3. Michele Harradence's first investor messaging as CEO in early 2027.
4. The next dividend declaration and any update to the growth outlook.
