## The short version: a defence giant takes the largest seat

MDA Space (TSX: MDA), the Brampton and Montreal company behind **Canadarm2** on the International Space Station and a growing satellite and geointelligence franchise, closed at **CA$41.34** on the October 8 screener, up about **15%** over twelve months and about **54%** year to date, after a softer **−19%** over three months. Then the ownership story changed. On **October 13, 2026**, Czech defence group **Czechoslovak Group (CSG)** settled a stake of about **9.5%**, becoming the **largest shareholder**. The new story is a **strategic defence investor** sitting beside a company that just posted **34% revenue growth** and still holds a **C$4.0 billion** backlog.

The proof number for a new buyer is the multiple. At about **52x trailing earnings** (forward closer to **29x**), MDA Space is not cheap versus its electronic-technology peers. Analysts still rate it a **Strong Buy**, with an average one-year target of **C$63.64** — about **54%** above the screener price. Below we go through the CSG stake, the Q2 print, and how MDA Space compares with Bombardier, Celestica, Kraken Robotics and Magellan Aerospace on size, returns, valuation and analyst targets.

## What happened: CSG takes 9.5% of MDA Space

On **October 13, 2026**, CSG, through its Mariselle unit, physically settled a share forward for **15,356,219** common shares at an average **C$42.6903**. That is about **9.5%** of shares outstanding and a stake worth roughly **US$434 million**, making CSG the **largest shareholder**. Investment Canada Act approval cleared the path for physical settlement after a cash-settlement-only structure.

CSG said the investment is its main entry into space, alongside smaller venture bets, and that it may explore:

- **European manufacturing or assembly** facilities with MDA Space,
- **partnering on acquisitions**,
- **introducing defence customers** to MDA Space as countries raise sovereign space and defence spending.

MDA Space's CEO welcomed CSG as a strategic investor. For equity holders, the near-term question is less about a new order tomorrow and more about whether a defence-industrial partner can open European doors that commercial satellite demand alone has not — a **strategic step, not an earnings jolt**.

### The Q2 print behind the stake

The stake lands on top of a strong operating print. On **August 7, 2026**, MDA Space reported second-quarter results for the period ended June 30:

- **Revenue C$498.6M, up 33.6%** year over year, with growth in Satellite Systems, Robotics & Space Operations and Geointelligence.
- **Adjusted EBITDA C$96.3M, up 26.2%**, with a **19.3%** margin inside the full-year 18–20% guide.
- **Backlog C$4.003B**, up **C$310M** from Q1 on **C$808.9M** of new bookings (backlog is still below the year-ago level after converting prior work).
- **2026 revenue guide narrowed to C$1.8–1.9B** (from C$1.7–1.9B), with adjusted EBITDA C$330–370M.
- **Net cash** of about **C$153M** after the March **NYSE listing**, with total liquidity about **C$1.1B**.
- **Free cash flow was negative** in the quarter on working-capital swings and factory expansion in Montreal.

Management also pointed to early **CHORUS** earth-observation contracts, Canadarm3 work for the Canadian Space Agency, and announced acquisitions of **Blue Canyon Technologies** and **CLS** to widen the satellite and services pipeline. Those deals add addressable market — and execution and dilution risk after the U.S. listing. Negative free cash flow and a backlog that is still below last year are the weak spots that feed the valuation discussion below.

## Business overview: MDA Space vs electronic-technology peers

MDA Space designs and builds **robotics for space**, **communications satellites**, and **geointelligence** products (including RADARSAT heritage). It sells to government and commercial customers in Canada, the U.S., Europe and Asia. Robotics (Canadarm lineage) is the brand story; satellites and earth observation are where a lot of the recent growth and backlog sit.

On the TSX screener, MDA Space sits in **Electronic technology**. **Bombardier (BBD.A)** is the large aerospace and defence peer in the same sector. **Celestica (CLS)** is the sector's electronics-manufacturing giant. **Kraken Robotics (PNG)** is a smaller marine-robotics name. **Magellan Aerospace (MAL)** builds aerostructures and engines-related products. We left out Evertz (broadcast gear), Firan and microcaps, and CAE (Commercial services in the screener).

## Why it matters: from Canadarm contractor to defence-backed space platform

The old narrative was MDA Space as a **Canadarm contractor** winning satellite work and listing in New York. The new narrative is a **Czech defence giant** as largest owner, while backlog holds near **C$4B** and MDA Space builds Montreal manufacturing and **CHORUS** earth observation.

The CSG stake fits that new narrative. It is not an order print on its own, but it signals who wants a seat at the table as sovereign space and defence budgets rise. The open question is whether European defence demand and the Blue Canyon / CLS pipeline can justify a **52x** trailing multiple while free cash flow stays negative.

## Bull case

1. **Strategic owner.** CSG's **9.5%** stake (~**US$434M**) is a vote of confidence, with possible European plants and defence-customer introductions.
2. **Growth and backlog.** Revenue grew about **34%** last quarter, backlog is **C$4.0B** (up **C$310M** vs Q1), and the midpoint of 2026 revenue guidance was raised into the **C$1.8–1.9B** range.
3. **Street upside.** Twelve analysts' average target near **C$64** and a Strong Buy tilt still imply **more than 50%** upside from the screener price (**C$41.34**).

## Bear case and risks

1. **Rich multiple.** Trailing P/E near **52x** sits well above the peer average near **36x**, and free cash flow is still negative on working capital and factory spending.
2. **Backlog and program risk.** Backlog is below last year's level, and large programs can slip or get cut (as with a River-class destroyer scope trim).
3. **Integration load.** Closing **Blue Canyon** and **CLS**, plus a new strategic shareholder after the U.S. listing, adds execution and dilution risk.

## What to watch

1. **CSG cooperation news.** Any follow-on announcements on European manufacturing or assembly facilities with MDA Space.
2. **Q3 2026 results.** Whether backlog stays near **C$4B** after Lightspeed and Canadarm3 work, and whether free cash flow improves.
3. **Deal closings.** Blue Canyon and CLS closing timelines, and cash burn versus the raised revenue guide.
4. **Analyst targets vs price.** Whether the stock closes the gap toward the **C$63.64** average target, or the Street revises after the CSG stake.

## Bottom line

MDA Space has earned attention: **34%** Q2 revenue growth, a **C$4.0B** backlog, a U.S. listing, and now a Czech defence giant as largest shareholder at about **9.5%**. The issue is price. At about **52x** trailing earnings, with free cash flow still negative and the stock already up about **54%** year to date, a lot of the growth story is in the multiple — even with a **Strong Buy** consensus and a **C$63.64** average target implying about **54%** upside from **C$41.34**. The next test is whether Q3 and any CSG cooperation news turn backlog and European defence interest into cash, not just a richer narrative.
