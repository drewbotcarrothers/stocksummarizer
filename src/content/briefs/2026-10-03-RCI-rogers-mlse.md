## What happened (news + tape)

On **October 1, 2026**, Rogers Communications completed the acquisition of the remaining **25%** ownership stake in **Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment (MLSE)** from **Kilmer Sports Inc.** for **C$4.35 billion**, bringing Rogers’ ownership of MLSE to **100%**.

- MLSE includes the Toronto Maple Leafs (NHL), Toronto Raptors (NBA), Toronto FC (MLS), Toronto Argonauts (CFL), development teams, Scotiabank Arena, and its Live Nation partnership.
- Rogers will create a **Rogers Sports** unit combining MLSE with the Toronto Blue Jays, Rogers Centre, Sportsnet, 30+ television networks and 40+ radio stations.
- **Keith Pelley** remains MLSE President & CEO and takes additional Rogers Media accountability; **Mark Shapiro** remains Blue Jays President & CEO.
- This follows Rogers’ 2025 purchase of Bell’s former ~37.5% MLSE stake (~C$4.7B) that made Rogers the majority owner, plus the July path to exercise the Kilmer option.

Street consensus on the more liquid **Class B** listing averages about **C$59.31** from **18 analysts** (Buy; S&P Global via stockanalysis, pulled October 3, 2026). National Bank cut its target from **C$62 to C$59** on September 30 (still Buy). Screener rating on RCI.A is **Buy**. The October 2 Class B close was **CA$43.94** versus the screener Class A print of **CA$44.99**.

## Bull / Bear / Watch

**Bull:** 100% of MLSE plus Blue Jays and Sportsnet under Rogers Sports after a clean October 1 close; Street still near C$59 with a Buy bias; forward ~9.5x with a ~4.45% indicated yield and ~17.5% payout.

**Bear:** C$4.35B on top of the earlier Bell stake buy raises leverage and sports/media execution risk; 1M still −13.5% with Bell and TELUS also soft; trailing ~4x is not a free lunch.

**Watch:** Rogers Sports operating model and Pelley’s media remit; next earnings (wireless/cable + any sports contribution); leverage and free cash flow after the cheque; whether Street holds near C$59.
