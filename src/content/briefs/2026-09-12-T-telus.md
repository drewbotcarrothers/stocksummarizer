## What happened (news + tape)

On **July 31, 2026**, TELUS reported Q2 and reset capital returns under new CEO **Victor Dodig**: dividend cut ~**55%** to annualized **CA$0.75**, ~**CA$2.7B** cash savings through 2028 for debt reduction, DRIP discount ending Oct 1, Q2 net loss ~**CA$1.8B** on a ~**CA$2.1B** TELUS Digital impairment, and lower FY26 guidance (FCF ~**CA$1.8B**). Leverage **3.5×** with a **≤3.0× by YE2028** target.

The **Sept 12 screener** still shows a weak chart (~**−43%** 1Y) and TTM FCF ~**CA$2.17B**. Old ~**13%** trailing yield on the screen is **superseded** by the reset (~**6%** indicated on ~CA$12.60).

## Why it matters

This is no longer “sleepy high-yield telecom.” It’s a **balance-sheet and capital-allocation reset**: cut the payout, kill the DRIP discount, write down Digital, lower FCF guidance, and push leverage repair into 2028. DIY investors who owned T for the dividend need the new math; growth/quality investors need to decide if Dodig’s three priorities (financial foundation, operational discipline, profitable growth in wireless/fibre/AI infra) can re-rate the stock after a multi-year drawdown.

## Bull case

- Management is finally prioritizing **deleveraging** with a clear cash savings number (~**CA$2.7B** through 2028) and an explicit **3.0×** leverage destination.
- Core wireless still growing network revenue; PureFibre build nearing completion implies lower capital intensity later.
- TTM FCF remains material; new FCF payout band **45–60%** is more conservative than 60–75%.
- Valuation on the screen (P/S ~**1×**, P/B ~**1.5×**, forward P/E ~**17×**) is not nosebleed if earnings stabilize.

## Bear case

- Dividend cut + guidance cut + Digital impairment = trust shock; income holders may keep selling.
- Competitive wireless pricing and slower population growth compress ARPU and net adds.
- Leverage still **3.5×**; path to 3.0× depends on FCF, lower capex intensity, and asset sales executing.
- Screen tape remains weak (Sell / Strong sell MAs); multi-month/YTD drawdowns are severe.

## What to watch next

1. Whether the stock stabilizes after digesting the **55%** dividend reset.  
2. Q3 (November) capital-returns framework update Dodig flagged.  
3. Progress vs **3.0×** leverage and any Health / real-estate monetization proceeds.  
4. Wireless ARPU, churn, and net adds vs Rogers/BCE competitive intensity.  
5. Digital turnaround (or further restructuring) after the impairment.
