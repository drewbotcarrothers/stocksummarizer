## The short version: Skouries turns the corner from building to running

Eldorado Gold (TSX: ELD), the miner with gold operations in Quebec, Greece and Türkiye, closed at **CA$44.32** on Friday, October 23, after a rough month: the stock fell about **28%** over the past month as the gold price pulled back from its highs. On the evening of **October 26, 2026**, Eldorado said the first two of six tailings filter presses are running at **Skouries**, its new copper-gold mine in northern Greece. The stock jumped **8.1% to C$48.03** on Tuesday, October 27.

That matters because the filtered tailings plant had been the project's critical path, and Skouries is the asset that is supposed to turn Eldorado from a company spending heavily on construction into one generating cash from a second metal. On expected earnings, Eldorado is the cheapest stock in its peer group at about **7x**, and it trades at about **1.2x book**. The open questions are whether the ramp-up stays on schedule and whether rising costs eat into the gold price. Below we go through the milestone, the second-quarter numbers behind it, and how Eldorado compares with Agnico Eagle, Barrick, Kinross, Alamos Gold and Lundin Gold on size, returns, valuation, dividends and analyst targets.

## What happened: the first filter presses start up at Skouries

On **October 26, 2026**, after the market closed, Eldorado announced that the first two tailings filter presses at Skouries are operational.

- **What a filter press does:** it squeezes water out of mine waste so the waste can be stacked as a dry material instead of being held behind a tailings dam. Filtered tailings are a core part of the Skouries design and of Eldorado's waste-management plan in Greece.
- **How many are needed:** the facility has **six** Metso filter presses, five operating units plus one installed spare. **Four** are needed for commercial production on sulphide ore and **five** for nameplate throughput.
- **What it means today:** with two presses running, the plant can operate continuously and is raising throughput as commissioning continues.
- **What comes next:** Eldorado expects the remaining **four** presses to finish commissioning in **November 2026**, with commercial production still targeted for the **fourth quarter**.

Chief executive **Christian Milau**, who took over from George Burns on September 30, called it "an important commissioning milestone." The update follows a busy autumn at the site: first copper-gold concentrate in early September, completion of site energization on September 28, and designation as a Strategic Project under the EU Critical Raw Materials Act on October 9. In the July report, the filtered tailings plant was still described as the project's critical path, which is why this step drew such a strong reaction.

Over a roughly **20-year mine life**, Skouries is expected to average about **140,000 ounces of gold and 67 million pounds of copper** a year. An updated technical report is expected in **December 2026**.

### The Q2 2026 print behind the milestone

Eldorado last reported on **July 30, 2026** (all figures in US dollars):

- **Gold production of 104,616 ounces**, down from 133,769 a year earlier, mainly because of planned lower output at Kışladağ in Türkiye.
- **Revenue of US$487.5M**, up from US$451.7M, on an average realized gold price of **US$4,379** an ounce.
- **Net earnings of US$172.8M** (US$0.69 a share) and **adjusted net earnings of US$136.7M** (US$0.54).
- **All-in sustaining costs of US$1,926** an ounce, up from US$1,520 a year earlier, as royalties, labour and maintenance costs rose and fewer ounces were sold.
- **Free cash flow of −US$334.1M**, driven by spending on Skouries and the new **McIlvenna Bay** copper-zinc mine in Saskatchewan, which came with the Foran Mining acquisition in April. Excluding those two projects, free cash flow was **US$40.9M**.
- 2026 gold production guidance of **495,000 to 600,000 ounces**, weighted to the second half, including **60,000 to 100,000 ounces** from Skouries.

The quarter shows the trade-off clearly. The existing mines are profitable at today's gold price, but the company has been pouring cash into two new mines at once. Skouries reaching commercial production is the step that should flip that spending into cash generation.

## Business overview: Eldorado Gold vs gold producer peers

Eldorado runs gold mines in Türkiye (Kışladağ and Efemçukuru), Quebec (the Lamaque complex) and Greece (Olympias), and it is building Skouries, a copper-gold mine in Halkidiki. McIlvenna Bay adds copper and zinc in Saskatchewan. Skouries is the biggest swing factor because it adds a long-life copper stream to what has been a gold-only story.

For peers we used liquid TSX gold producers from the same screener sector (Non-energy minerals): Agnico Eagle (AEM), Barrick (ABX), Kinross (K), Alamos Gold (AGI) and Lundin Gold (LUG). We left out the streaming and royalty companies, silver names and base-metal miners, which have different business models.

## Why it matters: from a construction bill to a cash story

The **old narrative** was a big construction bill in Greece. For years, Skouries was a project that ate cash, slipped behind schedule and kept the stock tied to execution risk, while the existing mines carried the company.

The **new narrative** is a company moving from spending cash toward making it. With the filter presses starting up and commercial production targeted for this quarter, Skouries is close to contributing gold and copper rather than consuming capital, and copper adds a second metal that does not depend only on the gold price. If the ramp-up goes to plan, the free-cash-flow picture could change quickly, and the low forward multiple suggests the market is not yet paying for that.

## Bull case

1. **A long-life gold and copper stream.** Skouries is designed to run for about 20 years, and spending should fall sharply once it reaches commercial production.
2. **Cheapest on expected earnings.** At about 7x forward earnings and 1.2x book, Eldorado is the lowest-priced name in this group on both measures.
3. **Cash is already going back to shareholders.** A new quarterly dividend and buybacks earlier this year show management is ready to return capital as the build phase ends.

## Bear case and risks

1. **Ramp-ups slip.** Skouries has been delayed before, and four more presses still need to be commissioned before commercial production.
2. **Costs are rising.** All-in sustaining costs were about **US$1,926** an ounce last quarter, up about **27%** from a year earlier, as royalties and labour costs climbed.
3. **The stock moves hard with gold.** Eldorado fell about 28% in a month when gold pulled back; a deeper pullback in gold prices would hit it again.

## What to watch

1. **Third-quarter results** after the close on **Thursday, October 29, 2026**, with a conference call on Friday, October 30 at 11:30 AM ET.
2. **The last four filter presses**, due to finish commissioning in **November 2026**.
3. **A declaration of commercial production at Skouries**, targeted for the **fourth quarter**.
4. **An updated Skouries technical report**, expected in **December 2026**.

## Bottom line

Eldorado has spent years building Skouries, and the October 26 filter-press update is the clearest sign yet that the project is moving from construction into production. The stock is cheap on expected earnings and on book value, it pays a small new dividend, and analysts see meaningful upside. The catch is that the cash has not shown up yet: trailing free cash flow is negative, costs are rising, and the stock swings hard with the gold price. Thursday's third-quarter report and the November commissioning of the remaining presses are the next tests of whether Skouries delivers on schedule.
