## What happened (news + tape)

On **September 14, 2026**, BCE / Bell signed a **non-binding MOU** with Saskatchewan for up to **+900 MW** on top of **300 MW** under contract (CoreWeave, Cerebras) — a path toward **~1.2 GW**. Q2 (Aug 6): revenue **+1.5%**, adj. EBITDA **+1%**, adj. EPS **+3.2%**, but **Free Cash Flow −9.5%** as Cap-X **+41.5%** for AI Fabric + Ziply FTTP. FY26 guide reaffirmed: FCF **CA$2.1–2.3B**, capital intensity **~20%**, dividend annualized **$1.75**.

The **Sept 18 screener** shows CA$31.20, soft multi-horizon tape (**−51.6%** 5Y), indicated yield ~**5.6%**. Screen DPS annual **2.31** is **stale**; trailing P/E ~**4.6** is **distorted** — prefer forward P/E ~**12.5**.

## Why it matters

BCE is selling a **second act**: keep the fibre/wireless cash engine, fund a large **AI Fabric / data-centre** build (Saskatchewan + partners), and still pay a **~5.6%** indicated dividend after the reset. DIY income holders care whether Cap-X intensity (~**20%** guided) and ~**CA$41B** net debt leave enough Free Cash Flow. Growth/AI narrative buyers care whether the **1.2 GW** path is real contracted MW or mostly MOU optionality.

## Bull case

- **AI Fabric scale narrative:** path to **1.2 GW** with named hyperscale-style tenants already on the first **300 MW**; first tenant prepayments already in.
- Core ops still grinding: Q2 rev/EBITDA/adj EPS up; fibre adds and **+14%** internet revenue; churn contained.
- Valuation not extreme on forward metrics (fwd P/E ~**12.5**, EV/EBITDA fwd ~**6.8**, P/S ~**1.2**).
- Indicated **~$1.75** dividend / ~**5.6%** yield is clearer post-reset than stale trailing prints.
- Analyst targets (**CA$40–43**) imply meaningful upside if AI Cap-X converts to contracted cash flows.

## Bear case

- Cap-X surge (**+41.5%** in Q2; ~**20%** intensity guide) presses Free Cash Flow (**−9.5%** Q2; FY FCF guide **CA$2.1–2.3B**).
- Balance sheet heavy: debt/equity ~**1.75**, net debt ~**CA$41.3B** vs equity ~**CA$24.2B**.
- **+900 MW** MOU is **non-binding** — needs customers, power, permits; **>CA$50B** framing is multi-decade and speculative.
- Tape and technicals weak (1D Strong sell; multi-horizon drawdowns; **−51.6%** 5Y).
- DPS growth YoY ~**−42%** hangover still colours income optics; trailing EPS/P/E distorted by one-offs.

## What to watch next

1. Conversion of Saskatchewan **MOU MW** into signed contracts, power agreements, and permits.  
2. H2’26 AI data-centre Cap-X spend (~**CA$1.3B** SK) vs tenant prepayments / setup fees.  
3. Whether FY26 FCF lands in the **CA$2.1–2.3B** guide with ~**20%** capital intensity.  
4. Dividend sustainability at annualized **$1.75** through the AI build cycle.  
5. Wireless churn/ARPU and fibre net adds vs Rogers/TELUS competitive intensity.  
6. Any update on Ziply Fiber US FTTP returns vs spend.
