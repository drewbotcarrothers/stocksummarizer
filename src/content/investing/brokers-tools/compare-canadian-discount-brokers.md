Comparing Canadian discount brokers for stock pickers should start with **your research workflow**, not a YouTube “best broker 2026” thumbnail. Commission-free trading did not make all-in costs equal — FX conversion, spreads, data limits, and transfer friction still matter.

## Direct answer

Score brokers on: (1) all-in trading costs including FX, (2) account lineup (TFSA/RRSP/FHSA/taxable/margin), (3) order and data tools you will actually use, (4) export/research hygiene, (5) transfer and funding friction. Rank for *your* process — not someone else’s day-trading style.

## Criteria that matter for stock pickers

### 1. All-in cost, not headline commission

- Equity commissions (often $0 now) vs ticket fees on some products
- **FX conversion** when buying USD names — frequently the largest silent cost
- Spreads on less-liquid TSX/TSXV names
- Inactivity, wire, or partial-fill oddities (read the fee schedule)

### 2. Accounts and registration fit

Confirm the broker supports the accounts you use and the securities you need (including any US-listed dual names). Transferring later is possible but not free in time or paperwork.

### 3. Order types and reliability

Limit orders, GTC behaviour around corporate actions, and mobile vs desktop parity. Stock pickers who trade around earnings care about outages and quote quality more than fancy skin themes.

### 4. Research and export

Can you export trades and tax lots? Is Level 2 noise or useful? Do you get filings links or only product push inside “education” centres? Prefer workflows that send you back to SEDAR+ and your own notes.

### 5. Screener and third-party stack

Many DIY investors pair a discount broker with TradingView, spreadsheet models, and issuer IR. Judge the broker as custody + execution; judge screeners separately (future spoke posts).

## A simple scorecard you can copy

| Criterion | Weight (example) | Notes |
|:--|--:|:--|
| FX + all-in cost | 25% | Especially if you buy USD |
| Account + transfer fit | 20% | TFSA/RRSP/FHSA needs |
| Order tools / stability | 20% | Your real usage |
| Export / tax-lot clarity | 15% | ACB sanity |
| Data / research hygiene | 10% | Avoid product push |
| Service / platform comfort | 10% | Subjective but real |

Re-weight freely — income investors who rarely touch USD should not overweight FX the same way dual-list growth buyers do.
