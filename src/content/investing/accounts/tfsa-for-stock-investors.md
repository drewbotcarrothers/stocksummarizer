A **Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA)** shelters Canadian-taxable growth and eligible Canadian investment income from Canadian tax inside the account. For stock pickers, the TFSA is powerful — and easy to misuse as a day-trading sandbox.

Educational investor-angle literacy only — not tax advice. Confirm rules with CRA materials or a qualified professional for your situation.

## Direct answer

Versus ETF-only holders, stock investors in a TFSA add **idiosyncratic risk**, more trading temptation, and more account-location decisions (especially for US dividend payers). The contribution room and withdrawal/recontribution mechanics stay the same; the behaviour and withholding details change.

## What stays the same

- Contribution room rules and unused room carry-forward
- Withdrawals freeing room on the next calendar year (mechanical reminder — verify current CRA guidance)
- Qualified investment constraints (penny-stock and exotic structure caution still applies)

## What changes for stock pickers

### 1. Behavioural risk rises

Commission-free trading plus a “tax-free” mental account can create overtrading. Frequent business-like trading inside a TFSA is an educational risk topic — see process hubs on TFSA trading risk. Keep an investment policy, not a dopamine loop.

### 2. US dividends and withholding

US-source dividends paid into a TFSA are often subject to non-recoverable withholding under common treaty practice for that account type — which is why many DIY investors prefer RRSP location for US dividend payers when the facts fit. Always verify treaty/withholding details for your holdings.

### 3. Concentration and Canada tilt

A TFSA full of three Canadian banks plus two producers is still a concentrated bet even if the account is tax-advantaged. Tax shelter ≠ diversification.

### 4. Record-keeping still matters

You need trade logs for your process even when the TFSA slip complexity differs from taxable ACB tracking. Kill criteria and thesis notes belong with the position, not only in the broker’s average-cost display.

## Practical checklist

- [ ] Satellite stock sleeve sized on purpose
- [ ] US dividend location considered
- [ ] No “TFSA day-trading” habit
- [ ] Thesis docs tied to each name
- [ ] Room tracked before large buys / transfers
