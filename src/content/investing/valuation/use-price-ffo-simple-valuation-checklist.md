P/FFO is quick to calculate and easy to misread. A checklist helps turn it into a price range you set before you look at the quote: gate the REIT on FFO quality and balance sheet first, then choose a fair multiple from history, peers and a simple yield-plus-growth check, then apply it to forward FFO in three scenarios.

This page pulls together the worksheet in [how to calculate price/FFO in CAD from filings](/investing/valuation/calculate-price-ffo-in-cad-from-filings/), the traps in [when price/FFO misleads](/investing/valuation/when-price-ffo-misleads-tsx/) and the peer method in [price/FFO vs peers](/investing/valuation/price-ffo-vs-peers-canada-first-comparison/). It is a framework for your own research, not a recommendation. All figures are hypothetical.

## Direct answer

(1) **Gate:** use P/FFO only for REITs and other real-estate owners that report FFO. (2) **FFO quality:** REALPAC basis, AFFO conversion understood, per unit including LP units. (3) **Balance sheet:** leverage and near-term maturities acceptable, with FFO adjusted for refinancing. (4) **Fair band:** from the REIT's own history, same-type peers and a yield-plus-growth cross-check. (5) **Price range:** forward FFO per unit × the band in bear, base and bull cases. Then check the result against P/AFFO, P/NAV and the distribution.

## The checklist

| Step | Question | Pass if |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 1. Gate | Is it a REIT or real-estate owner that reports REALPAC FFO? | Yes |
| 2a. Definition | Any material adjustments beyond REALPAC? | None, or removed |
| 2b. Conversion | AFFO ÷ FFO known and in line with property type? | Yes |
| 2c. Units | Diluted, including Class B LP units? | Yes |
| 3a. Leverage | Debt ÷ gross book value in line with same-type peers? | Yes, or explained |
| 3b. Maturities | Debt maturing within two years repriced in forward FFO? | Done |
| 3c. Distribution | AFFO payout below about 100% on your capex basis? | Yes |
| 4. Fair band | Band set from history, peers and yield-plus-growth? | Written before checking the price |
| 5. Range | Bear, base and bull forward FFO × band? | Done |
| 6. Cross-check | P/AFFO and P/NAV consistent with the range? | No contradiction, or explained |

## Setting the fair band

Use three inputs and look for overlap.

| Input | Hypothetical result |
|:--|:--|
| The REIT's own P/FFO range over a full rate cycle | 11x to 15x |
| Same-type peer median (from the peer table) | 14.5x |
| Yield-plus-growth cross-check (below) | About 13.6x |
| **Band used** | **11x (bear) to 14x (bull), 12.5x base** |

**Yield-plus-growth cross-check.** If a REIT pays out or reinvests its AFFO at its required return, a rough fair multiple of FFO is:

| Formula | Fair P/FFO ≈ (AFFO ÷ FFO) × (1 + g) ÷ (r − g) |
|:--|:--|
| AFFO ÷ FFO | 0.80 |
| Long-run FFO per unit growth, g | 2% |
| Required return, r | 8% |
| Result | 0.80 × 1.02 ÷ 0.06 ≈ **13.6x** |

The required return and growth here are illustrative; use your own. The formula is very sensitive to r − g (at r = 9%, the result drops to about 11.7x), which is why it is a cross-check, not the answer. The logic follows the dividend discount model in [how to build a simple DCF without fake precision](/investing/valuation/simple-dcf-without-fake-precision/).

## Cross-checks

| Check | Hypothetical result | Read |
|:--|:--|:--|
| P/AFFO at the base price | 16.3x on $0.95 AFFO | Close to the peer median P/AFFO |
| P/NAV at the base price | 0.95x | No large discount or premium |
| Implied cap rate at the base price | Close to recent transaction cap rates the REIT cites | Consistent |
| AFFO payout | 88% | Covered, with little room |
| Bear-case leverage | Debt ÷ GBV under 50% | Manageable |

If the cross-checks point the other way (a cheap P/FFO but a high P/AFFO, or a large NAV premium), go back to the capex and leverage gates before trusting the range. [Valuation for REITs](/investing/valuation/valuing-reits-nav-ffo-affo/) shows how to work out the implied cap rate.
