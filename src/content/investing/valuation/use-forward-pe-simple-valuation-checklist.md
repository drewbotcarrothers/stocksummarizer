Forward P/E is useful only when you know **how good the estimate is**. This checklist adds an estimate-quality step and a scenario step to the basic P/E process, so a single optimistic number does not decide your view.

Use it after the [simple P/E valuation checklist](/investing/valuation/use-pe-simple-valuation-checklist/), which covers trailing EPS quality, history and peers. It is educational; the example is hypothetical and does not value any company.

## Direct answer

Score the **estimate** first: source, coverage, revisions, EPS basis and assumptions. Then compare forward with **trailing P/E** to see the implied growth. Build **bear, base and bull** forward EPS (from guidance ranges or your own model) and apply a sensible multiple to each. Decide using the **bear case** as your margin-of-safety test: if the stock only works in the base or bull case, the low forward P/E is not enough on its own.

## The forward P/E checklist

| # | Check | Pass when | Read |
|--:|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | Period | NTM or a clearly named fiscal year | [What forward P/E means](/investing/valuation/what-forward-pe-means-canadian-diy/) |
| 2 | Source | Consensus from one provider, or your own model | [Calculate forward P/E](/investing/valuation/calculate-forward-pe-in-cad-from-filings/) |
| 3 | Coverage | Several recent estimates, not one or two | [Forward P/E vs peers](/investing/valuation/forward-pe-vs-peers-canada-first-comparison/) |
| 4 | Revisions | Stable or rising over 3–6 months | [When forward P/E misleads](/investing/valuation/when-forward-pe-misleads-tsx/) |
| 5 | Basis | You know if EPS is adjusted or IFRS | [Non-GAAP metrics](/investing/analyze/read-non-gaap-metrics-critically/) |
| 6 | Assumptions | Commodity, rate and FX inputs close to today | [Commodity sensitivity](/investing/analyze/read-commodity-price-sensitivity-resource-issuers/) |
| 7 | Implied growth | Trailing ÷ forward − 1 is believable for this business | Below |
| 8 | Scenarios | Bear, base and bull EPS written down | Below |
| 9 | Decision | Bear-case value compared with today's price | Below |

## Estimate quality score (simple)

| Factor | 2 points | 1 point | 0 points |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Coverage | 5+ estimates | 3–4 | 1–2 |
| Revisions (3 months) | Up | Flat | Down |
| Basis | IFRS or reconciled adjusted | Adjusted, mostly reconciled | Unclear |
| Assumptions | Match today | Slightly stale | Clearly stale |
| Guidance record | Usually met | Mixed | Usually missed |

A score of 8–10 means the forward P/E is a fair starting point. Below 5, widen the scenario range or rely more on trailing and cash flow measures.

## Building the three scenarios

| Scenario | Where the EPS comes from |
|:--|:--|
| Bear | Low end of guidance, recent estimate cuts continued, or a weaker commodity or rate case |
| Base | Midpoint of guidance or consensus, after your checks |
| Bull | High end of guidance with margins holding |

Use the **same multiple** for all three at first, so the scenarios show earnings risk. Then, if needed, use a lower multiple in the bear case, because markets usually pay less when earnings fall.
