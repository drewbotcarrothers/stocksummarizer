Long-term liabilities are more than bank debt. Defined-benefit (DB) pensions, asset-retirement obligations (AROs), leases, and other provisions can claim cash for decades. DIY investors who ignore the pension note sometimes understate leverage — or overreact to an accounting deficit that is not an imminent cash crisis.

Educational only — not financial advice. Examples are **hypothetical**. Pair with [debt notes](/investing/analyze/read-debt-notes-covenant-language/).

## Direct answer

Open the employee-benefits / pension note. Separate defined-contribution plans (expense ≈ contributions) from defined-benefit plans (assets, obligations, assumptions). Note surplus or deficit and the discount-rate / longevity assumptions at a high level. Add other long-term items (leases under IFRS 16, AROs, decommissioning) to your claims-on-cash map. Prefer contribution schedules and funding disclosures over pension accounting expense alone when judging near-term pressure.

## DB vs DC at a glance

| Feature | Defined contribution | Defined benefit |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Employer promise | Contribute a set amount | Deliver a formula benefit |
| Balance-sheet complexity | Usually low | Assets vs obligation; actuarial |
| Main investor risk | Expense level | Underfunding + assumption drift |
| Cash focus | Contributions | Contributions + special payments |

## Hypothetical funding sketch

| Item (hyp.) | Amount | Note |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Defined-benefit obligation | $500M | Present value of promised benefits |
| Plan assets | $420M | Market value |
| Deficit | $80M | Not always due tomorrow |
| Annual cash contributions | $25M | Near-term claim |
| Discount rate used | 4.5% | Lower rates → higher obligation (all else equal) |
| Lease liabilities | $120M | Separate but real |
| ARO (undiscounted sketch) | Large / long-dated | Timing matters |

## Other LT liabilities to park beside pensions

| Item | Where | Investor habit |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Lease liabilities | IFRS 16 note / BS | Include in enterprise claims |
| Asset-retirement / decommissioning | Provisions note | Critical for energy / materials |
| Litigation provisions | Contingencies | Probability × amount, not panic |
| Deferred tax | Tax note | Usually not a near-term cash twin |
