Debt footnotes look dense. They are also where refinancing cliffs, floating-rate exposure, and covenant tripwires hide. DIY investors who only read "net debt / EBITDA" from a slide miss the **schedule and the rules**.

Educational only — not financial advice. Ladder amounts are **hypothetical**. Pair with [interest coverage](/investing/analyze/analyze-interest-coverage-levered-tsx/), [dilution risk](/investing/analyze/spot-dilution-risk-canadian-issuers/), and the [MD&A](/investing/analyze/how-to-read-mdna/).

## Direct answer

Open the borrowings / long-term debt note. List principal by maturity year (or bucket). Note fixed vs floating rates and secured vs unsecured. Read covenant language in plain English: what ratio or absolute test, how often tested, and what happens on a breach (default, springing collateral, dividend blocker). Reconcile the note with the MD&A liquidity discussion.

## Plain-English covenant types

| Type | Rough meaning | Investor habit |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Leverage ratio | Debt vs EBITDA / equity cap | Check definition of "EBITDA" in the credit agreement summary |
| Interest coverage | EBIT or EBITDA vs interest | Align with your own coverage calc |
| Minimum liquidity | Cash + undrawn facilities floor | Matters near a maturity wall |
| Negative pledge | Limits on new secured debt | Protects unsecured holders' position |
| Dividend / buyback restricted | Cash returns blocked if breached | Income thesis at risk |
| Change of control | Debt may accelerate on takeover | M&A / privatization angle |

## Hypothetical note skim

| Item (hyp.) | Detail | Your flag |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Drawn revolver | $50M @ floating | Rate risk if policy stays higher |
| Term loan | $200M due year 3 | Refi wall |
| Senior notes | $150M due year 8 | Longer runway |
| Lease liabilities | Separately disclosed | Still a claim on cash |
| Max net leverage covenant | 3.5x | Headroom vs current (your calc) |
| Dividend blocker | If leverage > 3.0x | Income thesis sensitivity |
