Guidance is not a promise in the contractual sense — but the **words** still matter. DIY investors get more from a commitment log than from a single beat/miss headline.

Educational only — not financial advice. Examples are **hypothetical**. Links to the commitment-log idea in [how to read an earnings call transcript](/investing/analyze/how-to-read-earnings-call-transcript/) and the guidance check in [how to read an earnings press release](/investing/analyze/read-earnings-press-release/).

## Direct answer

Each quarter, copy the prior guidance sentence next to the new one. Diff verbs (raise, cut, reaffirm, narrow, drop). Log metric, range, time horizon, and qualifiers. Next quarter, mark delivered / missed / restated. Canadian forward-looking information rules (NI 51-102 Part 4A) require reasonable basis and updates when appropriate — your log tracks whether management's own targets are stable.

## Diff patterns that matter

| Pattern | What to write in the log |
|:--|:--|
| Reaffirm but narrow to low end | Commitment quietly weakened |
| Metric disappears from outlook | Goalpost may have moved |
| New qualifier stack ("subject to...") | Softening |
| Raise with same metric definition | Stronger commitment — still not advice to buy |
| Cut after soft language last quarter | Soft language was a warning |
