High insider ownership feels comforting. It can also mean an illiquid float, related-party complexity, or a founder who will not sell into your thesis. Use the number — do not **overweight** it.

Educational only — not financial advice. Percentages mentioned as examples are **hypothetical**. For monitoring mechanics see [SEDAR+ alerts](/investing/analyze/sedar-plus-alerts-filing-types-monitoring/) and [SEDAR+ vs EDGAR](/investing/analyze/sedar-plus-vs-edgar-us-stocks/).

## Direct answer

Pull the insider / significant-shareholder table from the AIF or management information circular, note who is included, skim recent SEDI filings for buy/sell patterns, and check early-warning reports around the 10% threshold. Then give ownership a small weight next to cash flows, leverage, and segment trends — never let it veto or bless a thesis alone.

## Ownership reading grid

| Question | Why it matters | Where |
|:--|:--|:--|
| What % do insiders own? | Alignment vs control | AIF, circular, proxy |
| Who is "insider" here? | Definitions vary by table | Footnotes |
| Recent open-market buys/sells? | Behaviour vs static % | SEDI (NI 55-104) |
| Any early-warning filings? | Accumulations / dispositions at 10%+ | NI 62-104 / NI 62-103 |
| Related-party deals? | Ownership plus self-dealing risk | Circular, notes |
| Dual-class / special voting? | Economic % vs voting % | Share capital note, AIF |

## Weighting guide (literacy, not a formula)

| Evidence tier | Example | Suggested weight in DIY brief |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Business & cash flow | Segment margins, FCF | High |
| Balance sheet | Leverage, runway | High |
| Insider ownership level | 15% held by directors (hyp.) | Low-to-moderate context |
| Single SEDI buy | One Form after a dip | Very low |
| CEO interview claim | "We are buying personally" | Near zero until SEDI confirms |

Canadian insider reporting generally runs through SEDI under NI 55-104 (reporting deadlines include a common five-trading-day theme for many filings — confirm current instrument text). Early-warning requirements for significant acquisitions of voting securities sit in NI 62-104 / NI 62-103.
