A clean beat can hide a messy quarter. One-time items — impairments, restructuring charges, gains on asset sales, litigation settlements — can turn a soft operating result into a strong headline, or the reverse. On Canadian issuers the place to start is the **financial statements and notes**, not the adjusted-EPS bullet in the press release.

Educational only — not financial advice. All worked numbers below are **hypothetical**.

## Direct answer

Scan the income statement and cash flow statement for unusual lines (impairment, restructuring, gain/loss on disposal, other income/expense). Open the matching note. Ask three questions: Is it cash or non-cash? Is it likely to recur? Does management's non-GAAP measure treat it consistently with prior periods? Then log the item on your thesis scorecard before you trust any adjusted figure. Pair this with [reading non-GAAP metrics critically](/investing/analyze/read-non-gaap-metrics-critically/) and [reading the earnings press release](/investing/analyze/read-earnings-press-release/).

## Where one-time items show up

| Location | Typical labels | First check |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Income statement | Impairment of goodwill/PPE, restructuring, gain/loss on sale, litigation, FX remeasurement | Size vs operating income; is it inside or below operating profit? |
| Notes | Nature, amount, whether cash has been paid | Cash vs non-cash; multi-year program vs single event |
| Cash flow statement | Add-backs in operating; proceeds in investing | Did the gain bring cash? Did the charge use cash? |
| MD&A | Management's narrative and outlook bridge | Do they call it non-recurring while planning another round next year? |
| Press release non-GAAP | Items removed to get adjusted EPS/EBITDA | Prominence and reconciliation under NI 52-112 |

IAS 1 *Presentation of Financial Statements* requires that when items of income or expense are material, an entity disclose their nature and amount separately. Canadian reporting issuers also face continuous-disclosure expectations under NI 51-102 and non-GAAP rules under NI 52-112.

### Common categories (educational map)

| Category | Often non-cash? | Recurrence risk | Investor habit |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Asset / goodwill impairment | Usually non-cash | Medium — can signal the thesis broke | Treat as a flag on economics, not only as an add-back |
| Restructuring / severance | Mixed | High if transformation is multi-year | Map the cash spend by year |
| Gain on sale of assets | Cash may arrive in investing | Low if truly a one-off disposal | Do not annualize the gain into run-rate earnings |
| Litigation / settlements | Often cash | Variable | Check remaining contingencies in the notes |
| Acquisition / integration costs | Mixed | High while M&A continues | Separate deal costs from run-rate opex |
| Inventory write-downs | Non-cash until sold | Can repeat in weak demand | Watch gross margin recovery |
