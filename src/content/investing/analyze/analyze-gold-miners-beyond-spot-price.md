Gold miners are often treated as a bet on the gold price. The price matters, but two miners can react very differently to the same move. The gap comes from **costs, reserve life, jurisdiction, balance sheet and how much of the gold price reaches shareholders**. If you only watch spot gold, you miss most of what drives a miner's results.

This post is an **analysis checklist** for gold producers. It links our existing gold explainers instead of repeating them: [how gold miners make money](/investing/sectors/how-gold-miners-make-money/), [what AISC means](/investing/glossary/what-is-aisc/) and [how to read commodity price sensitivity](/investing/analyze/read-commodity-price-sensitivity-resource-issuers/). It is educational and does not rate any miner. All numbers are hypothetical.

## Direct answer

Look past spot gold with five checks: (1) **margin per ounce** (realized price minus all-in sustaining cost); (2) **reserve life and grade** trends; (3) **jurisdiction and mine concentration**; (4) **capital allocation**: growth capex, buybacks, dividends and M&A; and (5) the **sensitivity table** in the MD&A that shows how earnings or cash flow move with gold, currency and oil.

## What to read in a miner's filings

| Item | Where to find it | Why it matters |
|:--|:--|:--|
| AISC and cash cost per ounce | MD&A non-GAAP section | Margin and cost trend |
| Reserve and resource statement | AIF, NI 43-101 technical reports | Mine life and the price assumption behind it |
| Sensitivity table | MD&A | Effect of gold, USD/CAD and oil moves |
| Sustaining vs growth capex | MD&A capex table | What is needed to keep production flat |
| Hedging and streaming obligations | Financial statement notes | Share of upside that is already sold |
| Jurisdiction and permits | AIF risk factors | Tax, royalty and permitting risk |

AISC is a non-GAAP measure based on a World Gold Council guidance note. Companies can define it a little differently, so compare definitions before you compare numbers. See [how to read non-GAAP metrics critically](/investing/analyze/read-non-gaap-metrics-critically/).

## Checklist

| Step | Done when |
|:--|:--|
| Margin per ounce logged | Realized price minus AISC for several quarters |
| Reserve life noted | Years of reserves and the gold price used |
| Concentration noted | Share of output from the largest mine and country |
| Sensitivity read | Effect of a 10% gold move and a USD/CAD move |
| Capital allocation checked | Growth capex, dividends, buybacks, acquisitions |
| Hedges and streams noted | Any production already committed |
