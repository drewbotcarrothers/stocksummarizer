The management information circular (often called the proxy circular) is where Canadian issuers put the meeting agenda, director nominees, compensation disclosure, and significant-holder tables. It is a **governance filing**, not a marketing deck. Reading it well beats skimming the highlight reel in the press release.

Educational only — not financial advice. Figures in examples are **hypothetical**. Pair with the [Annual Information Form](/investing/analyze/how-to-read-annual-information-form/) and [insider-ownership literacy](/investing/analyze/analyze-insider-ownership/).

## Direct answer

Open the circular (Form 51-102F5 style package under NI 51-102). Map the agenda and each vote. Read director bios for independence, tenure, and skill mix. Skim compensation summary tables for salary vs equity vs options and the performance metrics used. Note significant holders and any shareholder proposals. Then cross-check related-party and control themes against the AIF and notes before you change a thesis.

## What the circular usually contains

| Section | Investor use | Watch-out |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Notice & agenda | Which items get a vote | Bundled resolutions; special meetings |
| Director nominees | Independence, skills, overboarding | Bios that list titles without relevant expertise |
| Compensation discussion | Alignment of pay with stated goals | Peer group cherry-picking; one-year metrics only |
| Equity plans / options | Dilution and overhang context | Large authorized pools vs actual grants |
| Significant shareholders | Control vs economic float | Dual-class voting skew |
| Shareholder proposals | Material governance pressure | Non-binding proposals still signal risk |
| Indebtedness / related-party | Self-dealing clues | Cross-link to financial-statement notes |

Canadian continuous disclosure for reporting issuers sits primarily in NI 51-102; the circular's prescribed content is commonly associated with Form 51-102F5 (confirm the current form text for your issuer type). Say-on-pay and other advisory votes appear when required or adopted — absence of a vote is itself a data point, not automatic bad news.

## Hypothetical director-skill skim

| Nominee (hyp.) | Independent? | Core skill claimed | Your note |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| A | Yes | Audit / IFRS | Supports audit committee |
| B | Yes | Industry ops | Relevant to main segment |
| C | No (CEO) | Operator | Expected; check other executive directors |
| D | Yes | Capital markets | Useful if leverage / M&A is central |
| E | Yes | HR / comp | Useful if pay design is contested |

Ask: does the board's disclosed skill matrix match the **risks that can kill your thesis** (commodity, regulation, tech disruption, leverage)? Gaps are clues, not automatic sell signals.
