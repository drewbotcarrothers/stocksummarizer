"Related-party transaction" is a phrase that triggers headlines. In IFRS reporting it is also a **normal disclosure category**: key management, controlling shareholders, affiliates, joint ventures. The literacy skill is reading IAS 24-style notes **without panic** — and without ignoring real self-dealing risk.

Educational only — not financial advice. Amounts in examples are **hypothetical**. Cross-link the [management information circular](/investing/analyze/read-management-information-circular/), [insider ownership](/investing/analyze/analyze-insider-ownership/), and [segment reporting](/investing/analyze/read-segment-reporting-canadian-filings/).

## Direct answer

Open the related-party note (and circular sections on indebtedness / interest of informed persons). List who the parties are and how they relate to the issuer. Note nature, amounts, and whether terms are described as arm's-length or how pricing is set. Judge materiality versus revenue, assets, and earnings. Separate one-off clean-up deals from recurring dependency. Escalate only when size, terms, or governance quality fail your threshold — not because the word "related" appears.

## What "related party" usually covers

| Party type | Example | Where disclosed |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Key management | CEO, directors | Comp note + circular |
| Controlling / significant holders | Parent, 10%+ holder entities | RPT note, AIF, circular |
| Subsidiaries / associates / JVs | Intra-group sales | Often consolidated away; associates remain |
| Close family of KMPs | Family-owned supplier | RPT note when material |

## Hypothetical triage table

| Item (hyp.) | Amount | Recurring? | Terms note | Triage |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Sales to 40%-owned associate | $8M of $200M revenue | Yes | "Market terms" stated | Watch — map dependency |
| Lease from CEO's Holdco | $1.2M / year | Yes | No benchmarking disclosed | Escalate — ask for terms |
| One-time asset sale to JV | $5M gain | No | Fair-value opinion cited | Log; don't annualize gain |
| Key-mgmt compensation | Per circular | Yes | Standard CD&A | Route to [comp alignment](/investing/analyze/evaluate-executive-compensation-alignment/) |
