Earnings **beats** and **misses** feel binary. Good process is not. After each print, update the **written thesis** with what actually changed — mix, margins, cash, guidance quality — then decide whether bull, bear, or kill cards move.

Educational only — not financial advice. Decomposition figures are **hypothetical**. Pair with [track thesis over quarters](/investing/analyze/track-stock-thesis-over-quarters/), [separate news from thesis](/investing/analyze/separate-news-from-thesis/), [CFO vs NI](/investing/analyze/read-cfo-vs-net-income/), and [earnings press release](/investing/analyze/read-earnings-press-release/).

## Direct answer

Log the print against *your* expectations (not only street theatre). Decompose the surprise into price, volume, mix, FX, and one-times. Check cash conversion and working capital. Update Bull / Bear / Watch and kill criteria explicitly. Act only if the thesis changed — many beats and misses are noise relative to a multi-quarter scorecard.
