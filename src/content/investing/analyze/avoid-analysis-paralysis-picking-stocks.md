Analysis paralysis looks like diligence. You rebuild the model a third time, wait for one more quarter, read one more forum thread, and never actually decide. The cost is real: time, attention and often a missed decision either way. The fix is to set **stopping rules** before you start, so research ends in a decision: *buy*, *watch* or *pass*.

Educational only, not financial advice. "Decide" here includes deciding not to buy. This post doesn't push you toward action. Pair it with [behavioural biases that hurt stock pickers](/investing/process/behavioural-biases-stock-pickers/) and [position sizing](/investing/process/position-sizing-diy-stock-pickers/).

## Direct answer

Before researching, set a **time box** (for example, six focused hours over two weeks), a short list of **tier-one sources** (filings, MD&A, one transcript) and **five questions** your research must answer. When the questions are answered, kill criteria are written and the time box is used up, you decide: buy (at a pre-set size), watch (with a trigger) or pass (with a reason). Remember that new information has diminishing returns, and that a written watch or pass is a complete outcome.

## Source tiers

| Tier | Sources | Use |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 (required) | Annual + latest interim filings, MD&A, AIF | Every decision |
| 2 (one each) | One earnings call transcript, one peer's filing | Context |
| 3 (optional, time-boxed) | Sell-side notes, industry reports | Only to check your thinking; see [triangulating sources](/investing/analyze/triangulate-filings-sell-side-notes-own-model/) |
| 4 (skip by default) | Forums, social media, price-target headlines | Sentiment only |

The ranking follows the [source hierarchy framework](/investing/frameworks/source-hierarchy-filings-transcripts-commentary/).

## Outcomes are decisions

| Outcome | What you write | Next step |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Buy | Thesis, kill criteria, size (pre-set), review date | Pre-earnings checklist next quarter |
| Watch | Trigger (price, KPI or event) and a review date | [Watchlist process](/investing/analyze/build-watchlist-process-not-noise/) |
| Pass | One-line reason | Revisit only if the reason changes |
