You don't need a whole evening to process a quarterly report. With a [pre-earnings checklist](/investing/analyze/build-pre-earnings-checklist/) already done, a disciplined **60-minute debrief** covers what matters: what changed, whether your thresholds were met and what you'll do. Unresolved questions become dated follow-ups instead of an evening of doom-scrolling.

Educational only, not financial advice. This is a timed workflow. For how to decide what a beat or miss means for your thesis, see [updating a thesis after a beat or miss](/investing/analyze/update-stock-thesis-after-beat-or-miss/). For document-level reading, see [quarterly earnings for TSX companies](/investing/analyze/how-to-read-quarterly-earnings-tsx/).

## Direct answer

Work in five timed blocks. Spend **10 minutes** on the press release (headline vs GAAP, guidance change). Spend **20 minutes** on the statements and MD&A, looking only at what changed since last quarter. Spend **10 minutes** filling your KPI scorecard against the thresholds you set beforehand. Spend **15 minutes** on the call or transcript, prioritizing the Q&A and any guidance language. Use the last **5 minutes** for the decision log: action (usually none), the rule that triggered it and the next review date.

## Block by block

| Block | Time | Focus | Skip |
|:--|--:|:--|:--|
| Press release | 10 min | GAAP vs adjusted, guidance change, one-offs | Management adjectives |
| Statements + MD&A | 20 min | Cash flow, working capital, debt, segment changes | Unchanged boilerplate |
| KPI scorecard | 10 min | Actual vs pre-set thresholds | New KPIs you didn't plan for (note them for later) |
| Call / transcript | 15 min | Q&A, guidance wording, capital allocation | Prepared remarks that repeat the release |
| Decision log | 5 min | Action, rule cited, next date | Price-target revisions |

## Scorecard template (hypothetical)

| KPI | Threshold band set beforehand | Actual | Band hit | Pillar affected |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Organic growth | Good ≥ 6% / Kill < 0% | 4.1% | OK | Growth |
| Gross margin | Good ≥ 38% / Kill < 34% | 35.2% | Concern | Pricing power |
| CFO vs net income | ≥ 1.0x | 0.7x | Concern | Earnings quality |
| Net debt / EBITDA | ≤ 2.5x | 2.6x | OK | Balance sheet |

Two "concern" bands don't trigger a kill, but they go into the [quarter-over-quarter log](/investing/analyze/track-stock-thesis-over-quarters/). A second consecutive concern on margins would prompt a full thesis review.

## What to check in the 20-minute block

- **Cash flow first:** [CFO vs net income](/investing/analyze/read-cfo-vs-net-income/) and the [FCF checklist](/investing/analyze/fcf-stock-decisions-quick-checklist/).
- **Working capital:** [inventory and receivables](/investing/analyze/analyze-inventory-turns-receivables-quality/) and [working capital swings](/investing/analyze/analyze-working-capital-swings-quarterly/).
- **One-time items:** [spot them at statement level](/investing/analyze/spot-one-time-items-canadian-statements/).
- **Share count:** [fully diluted share counts](/investing/analyze/read-cap-tables-fully-diluted-share-counts/) if buybacks or issuance are part of the story.
