Markets reward reaction. DIY research rewards **classification**. The same headline can be a real catalyst, background noise, or a quiet rewrite of your thesis — but you only know which if you already wrote the thesis down.

Educational only — not financial advice. Examples are **hypothetical**. This is a note-keeping workflow that links the [Thesis / Catalyst / Kill card](/investing/frameworks/thesis-catalyst-kill-criteria-card/) and the [Bull / Bear / Watch brief](/investing/analyze/stock-brief-template-bull-bear-watch/) — it does not replace them with a new decision card.

## Direct answer

Keep a one-page **thesis log** for each stock. When news hits, write what happened, map it to a named thesis pillar (or mark "none"), classify it as catalyst / noise / thesis change, and only then decide whether to act. If you cannot name the pillar, you are reacting to the tape, not updating a thesis.

## Why news and thesis get fused

| Trap | What it looks like | Fix |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Headline = thesis | "Stock up 8% so the story is working" | Price is not a pillar |
| Narrative drift | Quietly rewriting why you own it after every article | Diff against last quarter's written thesis |
| Catalyst inflation | Treating every rumour as a timed event | Catalysts need a date or a filing |
| Silence bias | Ignoring dull filings that actually matter | SEDAR+ alerts before Twitter |

## Thesis log template (keep it boring)

| Field | Example (hypothetical) |
|:--|:--|
| Ticker / as-of | ABC / 2026-11-05 |
| One-sentence thesis | Midstream volume growth funds 5% dividend growth without new equity |
| Pillars (3 max) | Contracted volumes; leverage under 4.0x; no common equity issue |
| Kill criteria | Leverage >4.5x for two quarters; guidance cut to flat volumes |
| Open catalysts | Q4 results date; regulator decision on expansion |
| Last news mapped | Broker note — noise (no pillar change) |

## News classification table

| News type | Usually maps to | Default class |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Earnings / MD&A / AIF | Cash flows, risk, timeline | Catalyst or thesis edit |
| Guidance change | Timeline / pillars | Thesis edit until proven |
| Sector move / macro | Often none | Noise unless thesis is macro |
| M&A rumour without filing | Sentiment | Noise until SEDAR+/press release |
| Insider Form / SEDI | Alignment signal only | Noise unless pattern + thesis link |
| Broker target change | Sentiment | Noise |
