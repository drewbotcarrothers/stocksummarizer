A thesis that only works in a soft-landing brochure is incomplete. **Stress-testing against a recession scenario** does not mean predicting GDP — it means asking what happens to *this* issuer's volumes, margins, covenants, and funding if demand drops for several quarters.

Educational only — not financial advice. Scenario figures are **hypothetical**. Pair with [interest coverage](/investing/analyze/analyze-interest-coverage-levered-tsx/), [debt notes](/investing/analyze/read-debt-notes-covenant-language/), [FCF checklist](/investing/analyze/fcf-stock-decisions-quick-checklist/), and [thesis tracking](/investing/analyze/track-stock-thesis-over-quarters/).

## Direct answer

Write the base thesis in one page. Choose a sector-plausible down case (not a cartoon apocalypse). Haircut volume, price, and margins with operating leverage in mind. Re-run cash: working capital, FCF, dividends, maturities, covenants. Then choose: thesis intact with scars, watch with triggers, or kill. Log the scenario next to [Bull / Bear / Watch](/investing/analyze/stock-brief-template-bull-bear-watch/).

## Minimum stress table

| Driver | Base (hyp.) | Recession (hyp.) | Source / assumption |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Volume | Flat | −20% | Sector analogue |
| Realized price | +2% | −5% | Promo / spot |
| Gross margin | 32% | 28% | Deleverage |
| EBITDA margin | 22% | 10% | Op. leverage |
| FCF | +$12 | −$2 | WC outflow |
| Covenant headroom | OK | Tight | Debt note |
| Action | Hold | Watch / size down | Per kill card |
