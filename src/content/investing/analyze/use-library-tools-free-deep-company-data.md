Paid research terminals cost thousands of dollars a year. Many public libraries in Canada license some of the same kinds of company data and make it free with a library card, often from home. For a DIY investor, that means **long financial histories, dividend records, industry reports and peer lists** without a subscription.

This post explains what these databases are good for, how to fit them into your research process and where to be careful. It is educational. Database names and availability change, and each library system licenses different products, so check your own library's list before you plan around any of them.

## Direct answer

Get a library card, open your library's **business and finance databases** page, and look for products such as **Financial Post (FP) Advisor** (Canadian companies), **Mergent**, **Value Line** (US-listed companies) and **MarketLine** or **Business Source Complete** (company and industry profiles). Use them for **long histories, dividend records, corporate history and peer lists**. Then confirm any number you rely on against the company's own filings on SEDAR+, because databases standardize and sometimes adjust figures.

## What each type of database can offer

| Database (examples) | Typical coverage | Useful for |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Financial Post (FP) Advisor | Canadian public and private companies, industry reports, dividends, predecessor and defunct companies | Ten-year histories, dividend records, name changes and corporate history |
| Mergent (Online or Intellect) | Global public companies, some private companies | Financial statements in a standard format, company histories |
| Value Line | US-listed companies only | One-page summaries, long histories, US peers of a TSX company |
| MarketLine | Global companies and industries | Industry structure, competitor lists, SWOT summaries |
| Business Source Complete | Business journals, trade press, company and industry profiles | Trade publications and background reading |

Toronto Public Library, for example, lists FP Advisor, Mergent, Value Line, MarketLine and Business Source Complete among its resources for DIY investors. Other library systems and university libraries offer different sets; some university databases are on-site only for visitors.

## Where library data fits in your process

| Research step | Library database role | Primary source to confirm |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Long-term history | Ten or more years of revenue, earnings and dividends | Annual reports for key years |
| Peer list | Industry classification and competitors | [Peer tables without cherry-picking](/investing/analyze/use-peer-tables-without-cherry-picking/) |
| Dividend record | Payment history, cuts and special dividends | Company press releases |
| Corporate history | Mergers, spin-offs, name changes | AIF and circulars |
| Industry context | Industry reports and trade press | MD&A outlook sections |
| US peer comparison | US company summaries | [EDGAR filings](/investing/analyze/sedar-plus-vs-edgar-us-stocks/) |

This order follows the site's [source hierarchy](/investing/frameworks/source-hierarchy-filings-transcripts-commentary/): filings first, databases as a fast index into them.

## Common traps

| Trap | How to avoid it |
|:--|:--|
| Standardized "EBITDA" or "cash flow" differs from the company's figure | Read the database definition; compare with the MD&A |
| Fiscal years do not line up across peers | Align by fiscal year end before comparing |
| Mixed currencies (CAD and USD) | Check the reporting currency; see [currency in CAD vs USD results](/investing/analyze/handle-currency-cad-vs-usd-results/) |
| Old data after a recent quarter | Check the "last updated" date |
| Database ratings or recommendations | Treat as one opinion; see [evaluating stock score apps critically](/investing/brokers-tools/evaluate-stock-score-apps-critically/) |
| Licence terms | Library licences usually allow personal research use only; do not republish data |

## Checklist

| Step | Done when |
|:--|:--|
| Library card active | Remote login works |
| Databases listed | You know which products your library offers |
| History pulled | Ten-year table saved with source and date |
| Key numbers verified | Matched against SEDAR+ filings |
| Notes updated | Source and retrieval date logged |
