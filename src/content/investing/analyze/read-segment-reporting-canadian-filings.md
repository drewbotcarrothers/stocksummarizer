Consolidated revenue can look fine while the business you thought you owned is shrinking. **Segment reporting** is how Canadian IFRS filers show the pieces — and it goes deeper than the short notes table in a general annual-report walkthrough.

Educational only — not financial advice. Mix figures below are **hypothetical**. For the broader annual-report reading order see [How to read a Canadian annual report](/investing/analyze/read-canadian-annual-report/).

## Direct answer

Open the IFRS 8 operating-segment note. Identify how management defines segments and segment profit, read the reconciliation from segment results to consolidated profit, then trend revenue and margin by segment for several years. Ask: which segment must work for my thesis to work?

## What IFRS 8 is asking for

| Concept | Plain English | Investor use |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Operating segments | Components reviewed by the chief operating decision maker | Match segments to how the company is actually run |
| Reportable segments | Those that meet quantitative thresholds (or are aggregated under rules) | Know when small pieces are hidden inside "Other" |
| Segment profit/loss | The measure management uses internally — may differ from IFRS operating income | Never assume it equals EBIT |
| Reconciliations | Bridge from segment totals to consolidated figures | Find corporate costs, eliminations, one-offs |
| Entity-wide disclosures | Products/services, geography, major customers | Concentration and geographic mix |

IFRS 8 *Operating Segments* is the core standard. Annual and interim packages for Canadian reporting issuers sit under NI 51-102 continuous disclosure.

## Reconciliation checklist

| Check | Why it matters |
|:--|:--|
| Segment profit definition stated clearly? | Apples-to-apples vs peers and vs prior years |
| Corporate / unallocated costs size | Can erase segment profits |
| Inter-segment eliminations | Inflated segment revenue if ignored |
| One-time items inside a segment | See [one-time items](/investing/analyze/spot-one-time-items-canadian-statements/) |
| Capex by segment (if given) | Growth vs harvest businesses |
| Geographic revenue vs segment revenue | Different cuts, both useful |
