Investors sometimes treat cash from operations (CFO) as "real earnings" and net income as "accounting fiction." That is too blunt. CFO and net income answer different questions. The skill is reading the **bridge** between them — working capital, non-cash items, and one-time cash effects — without worshipping either line.

Educational only — not financial advice. Worked numbers are **hypothetical**. See also [one-time items](/investing/analyze/spot-one-time-items-canadian-statements/), [non-GAAP literacy](/investing/analyze/read-non-gaap-metrics-critically/), and the [FCF hub](/investing/analyze/free-cash-flow-analysis/).

## Direct answer

Open the cash-flow statement. Start from net income (indirect method) or from cash receipts/payments (direct method). Identify non-cash add-backs (depreciation, impairments, stock-based compensation), working-capital movements, and classification of interest/tax paid. Compare CFO to net income over several periods. A persistent gap needs a story (growth investment, channel stuffing, payable stretch) before you rewrite a thesis.

## Bridge building blocks

| Bridge piece | Typical effect on CFO vs NI | Literacy habit |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Depreciation & amortization | CFO > NI (add-back) | Still need maintenance capex later for FCF |
| Impairments / provisions | Often non-cash add-back | Economics may still be broken |
| Stock-based compensation | Non-cash add-back | Dilution lives elsewhere |
| Δ Receivables / inventory / payables | Can dominate the quarter | See [working-capital swings](/investing/analyze/analyze-working-capital-swings-quarterly/) |
| Gains on asset sales | Often removed from operating | Cash may sit in investing |
| Interest & tax paid | Classification varies | Compare consistently across peers |

## Quality questions (not a score)

| Question | Better answer looks like | Redder answer looks like |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Is CFO ≈ NI over a cycle? | Gaps mean-revert with seasonality | CFO chronically below NI without growth WC |
| Are add-backs mostly D&A? | Routine | Ever-growing "other" add-backs |
| Is WC a one-quarter spike? | Explained by season / project | AR keeps climbing vs sales |
| Do one-times hit NI but not CFO? | You adjust both views | Management markets only the flattering view |
