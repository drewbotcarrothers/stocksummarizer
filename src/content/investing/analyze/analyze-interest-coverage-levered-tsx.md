Leverage stories on the TSX often stop at "net debt / EBITDA." **Interest coverage** asks a sharper question: can operating profit and operating cash cover the interest bill as rates, spreads, or refinancing terms move? For levered industrials, energy services, and some consumer names that question is central. For banks, insurers, and many REITs, the toolkit changes.

Educational only — not financial advice. Ratios below are **hypothetical**. Pair with [debt notes & covenants](/investing/analyze/read-debt-notes-covenant-language/) and valuation context such as [EV/EBITDA literacy](/investing/glossary/what-is-ev-ebitda/).

## Direct answer

Compute EBIT (or operating income) divided by interest expense for the same period. Add a cash variant: CFO (or CFO before interest/tax if you rebuild it carefully) versus cash interest paid from the cash-flow statement or notes. Read both across a few years. Then apply sector caveats — do not rank a Schedule I bank on industrial EBIT coverage. Stress coverage for a rate or refinancing shock near the maturity wall.

## Coverage toolkit

| Ratio | Numerator | Denominator | Use |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Accrual coverage | EBIT / operating income | Interest expense | Classic leverage lens |
| EBITDA coverage | EBITDA | Interest expense | Adds back D&A — easier, looser |
| Cash coverage | CFO (careful definition) | Cash interest paid | Reality check on accruals |
| Fixed-charge (advanced) | EBIT + lease-like | Interest + leases/rent | When leases are large |

## Sector caveats (TSX-relevant)

| Sector | Why standard EBIT coverage misleads | Better habit |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Banks | Interest is the business model | NIM, capital, credit — see bank explainers / primers |
| Insurers | Investment float & actuarial | Solvency / capital frameworks over EBIT/interest |
| REITs | AFFO/FFO and distributions | [FFO glossary](/investing/glossary/what-is-ffo/) + leverage to assets |
| Utilities | Regulated returns, long debt | Regulatory decisions + ladder |
| Cyclicals (energy, materials) | Peak EBIT flatters coverage | Mid-cycle stress |
