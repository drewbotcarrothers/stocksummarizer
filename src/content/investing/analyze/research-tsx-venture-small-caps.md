The TSX Venture Exchange (TSXV) lists early-stage and smaller companies, many in mining, energy and technology. The research process that works for a large bank or railroad on the TSX does not transfer cleanly. A venture issuer may have no revenue, no analyst coverage and only a few months of cash. The first question is usually **can this company fund itself until something good happens**, not what it is worth on a multiple.

This is a process guide for learning, not a recommendation of any listing. Numbers are hypothetical.

## Direct answer

Flip the order of your checklist. Start with **cash runway and financing history**, then **share structure and dilution**, then **people and insiders**, then the **asset or product**, and only then valuation. Venture issuers have lighter disclosure rules and longer filing deadlines, so read every filing you can find, set alerts, and size positions small enough that a total loss would not hurt your plan.

## What changes for venture issuers

| Area | Non-venture issuer (TSX) | Venture issuer (TSXV, CSE) | Research impact |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Annual statements deadline | 90 days after year end | 120 days | Older data when you read it |
| Interim statements deadline | 45 days | 60 days | Same |
| Annual Information Form | Required | Not required | Fewer business details; read the MD&A and news releases closely |
| CEO/CFO certification | Full certificates | Basic certificates (no ICFR or disclosure controls representations) | Less assurance on internal controls |
| Analyst coverage | Common | Rare | No consensus to check against |
| Liquidity | Usually deep | Often thin, wide bid-ask | Trading costs and exit risk |

## Research order for a venture name

| Step | Question | Where to look |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 1. Cash runway | How many months of cash at the current burn? | Latest statements: cash, working capital, operating cash flow |
| 2. Financing history | How often, at what prices, with how many warrants? | News releases, [prospectus filings](/investing/analyze/read-prospectus-secondary-offerings/), private placement notices |
| 3. Share structure | Basic vs fully diluted shares; escrowed shares | [Cap tables](/investing/analyze/read-cap-tables-fully-diluted-share-counts/); MD&A outstanding share data |
| 4. People | Track record, other boards, insider buying and selling | [Insider ownership](/investing/analyze/analyze-insider-ownership/); SEDI; the circular |
| 5. Asset or product | Is there a real, verifiable asset or customer? | Technical reports, contracts, customer names |
| 6. Valuation | Only now: what is priced in? | Peer comparisons with similar-stage companies |

## Tier and board signals

The TSXV has **Tier 1** and **Tier 2** issuers, with Tier 1 requiring stronger financial or asset tests. The **NEX** board holds companies that no longer meet ongoing listing requirements. Capital Pool Companies (CPCs) are shells that list to find a qualifying transaction. A move to NEX, a long-dormant CPC or repeated cease trade orders for late filings are worth noting before you go further.

## Common traps

| Trap | Why it hurts | Guardrail |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Paid promotion | Price runs on marketing, not results | Search for "paid advertisement" disclosures; check what new filings say |
| Warrant overhang | Warrants cap the price as holders exercise and sell | Count warrants and strike prices |
| Late filings | Can lead to a cease trade order | Track filing dates against deadlines |
| Position too large | Thin trading makes exits slow | Cap venture names in your [position sizing](/investing/process/position-sizing-diy-stock-pickers/) plan |

## Checklist

| Step | Done when |
|:--|:--|
| Runway calculated | Months of cash vs next milestone |
| Financing log built | Every raise in the past three years with price and warrants |
| Fully diluted count | Warrants, options and escrow included |
| Insider activity reviewed | SEDI filings for the past 12 months |
| Liquidity checked | Average daily value traded vs your position size |
| Exit rule written | What would make you sell, set before you buy |
