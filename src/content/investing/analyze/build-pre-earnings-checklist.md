Earnings day is when DIY investors make their worst decisions: rushed, emotional, reacting to a headline number and a price move. Do the thinking **before** the release. A pre-earnings checklist turns the day into a comparison exercise. You check the results against thresholds you wrote down when you were calm.

Educational only, not financial advice. This is the *before* half of a pair. The *after* half is [how to debrief an earnings print in under an hour](/investing/analyze/debrief-earnings-print-under-an-hour/). Reading guides for the documents themselves are in [quarterly earnings for TSX companies](/investing/analyze/how-to-read-quarterly-earnings-tsx/) and [the earnings press release](/investing/analyze/read-earnings-press-release/).

## Direct answer

One to two weeks before the release, confirm the date, re-read last quarter's MD&A and any guidance, and write down three to five KPIs with **pre-set thresholds** (good / OK / concern / kill). Re-read your kill criteria. Pre-commit to what you will and won't do on release day, such as no trades in the first hour and acting only when a written rule triggers. On the day, fill in the actual numbers and follow the debrief plan.

## The checklist

| # | Item | Done when |
|--:|:--|:--|
| 1 | Release date and call time confirmed | In your calendar, from the company's investor relations page |
| 2 | Last quarter's press release, MD&A and call notes re-read | You can state the three things management promised |
| 3 | Guidance and its assumptions logged | Written in your [guidance commitment log](/investing/analyze/evaluate-management-guidance-language/) |
| 4 | 3–5 KPIs chosen | Tied to your thesis pillars, not generic |
| 5 | Thresholds written for each KPI | Good / OK / concern / kill bands set |
| 6 | Kill criteria re-read | From your [Thesis → Catalyst → Kill card](/investing/frameworks/thesis-catalyst-kill-criteria-card/) |
| 7 | Known one-offs noted | Expected restructuring, FX, weather, strikes |
| 8 | Position size and max change set | Per your [position sizing rules](/investing/process/position-sizing-diy-stock-pickers/) |
| 9 | Release-day rules written | e.g. no trades for the first hour; read the filing before the headlines |
| 10 | Debrief time booked | Within 48 hours |

## Hypothetical threshold example

| KPI | Good | OK | Concern | Kill |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Organic revenue growth | ≥ 6% | 3–6% | 0–3% | < 0% two quarters running |
| Gross margin | ≥ 38% | 36–38% | 34–36% | < 34% with no stated one-off |
| Net debt / EBITDA | ≤ 2.5x | 2.5–3.0x | 3.0–3.5x | > 3.5x or a covenant amendment |

The numbers are hypothetical. What matters is writing them down before you see the results.
