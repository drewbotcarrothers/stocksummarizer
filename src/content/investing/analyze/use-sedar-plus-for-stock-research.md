**SEDAR+** is the official filing system for Canadian public companies and investment funds. For DIY TSX research it is the primary source layer — above apps, transcripts summaries, and social media.

Educational walkthrough only. Interfaces change; verify labels on the live site.

## Direct answer

Search the issuer on SEDAR+, open the profile, then read in this order for equity research: latest **AIF** → annual **MD&A** + financial statements → latest interim MD&A → material press releases / material change reports → management information circular when ownership or compensation matters.

## Why SEDAR+ beats secondary apps first

Secondary tools compress and restate. They are useful *after* you know what the company said about itself. SEDAR+ gives you timestamps, full risk factors, segment notes, and the exact non-GAAP reconciliations management wants you to see.

## Step-by-step workflow

### 1. Find the issuer

Use the SEDAR+ search for the company legal name or ticker. Confirm you have the correct reporting issuer (watch near-duplicate names and funds vs operating companies).

### 2. Orient on the profile

Note the reporting issuer status and recent filing activity. Heavy filing clusters often mark financings, M&A, or annual season.

### 3. Pull the Annual Information Form (AIF)

The AIF is the structured business and risk encyclopedia: corporate structure, operations, risk factors, legal proceedings, and often mineral or reserve disclosure for resource issuers. Skim risk factors with a highlighter mindset — what would actually change cash flows?

### 4. Read the annual MD&A with the statements

MD&A is management’s narrative over the numbers. Read it beside the financial statements, not instead of them. Track: revenue drivers, margin bridges, liquidity, debt, capex plans, and non-GAAP metrics with reconciliations.

### 5. Update with the latest interim package

Interims tell you whether the annual story is intact. Compare guidance language, working-capital swings, and one-time items.

### 6. Add circulars and material changes selectively

Proxy circulars matter for compensation alignment, related parties, and major votes. Material change reports matter when the press release is thin.

## Practical research hygiene

- Save PDFs with **date + form type** in the filename.
- Quote page numbers in your notes.
- When a metric appears only in a presentation, find it again in MD&A or notes before you trust it.
- For dual-listed names, still start Canadian for Canadian issuers; use EDGAR as a complement for US peers or US listings.

## Common DIY mistakes on SEDAR+

- Reading only the earnings release and skipping the MD&A.
- Ignoring AIF risk factors until after purchase.
- Treating non-GAAP “adjusted” earnings as cash without the reconciliation.
- Mixing preliminary short forms / prospectuses into operating thesis notes without labelling them as financing documents.
