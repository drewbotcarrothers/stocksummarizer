Canadian-listed retail and consumer companies range from grocers and pharmacies to discount chains, apparel brands, convenience operators and restaurant franchisors. They share a common analysis chain (stores, same-store sales, margins, costs, cash returns), but a Canadian footprint adds its own wrinkles: a small, spread-out population, long winters, imports priced in US dollars, provincial rules on alcohol, pharmacy and cannabis, and concentrated competition in grocery.

This is an **analysis checklist**. It links our deeper posts on [same-store sales](/investing/analyze/analyze-same-store-sales-volume-metrics/), [inventory and receivables](/investing/analyze/analyze-inventory-turns-receivables-quality/) and [gross margin trends](/investing/analyze/analyze-gross-margin-trends/) rather than repeating them. It is educational only.

## Direct answer

Start with **where the money is earned** (Canada vs US vs elsewhere), then read **same-store sales split into traffic and basket**, **gross margin drivers** (mix, shrink, currency on imported goods), **lease and wage costs**, and **inventory health**. Check the balance sheet for **lease liabilities** (IFRS 16), any **credit card or financial services arm**, and the **loyalty program**. Finish with how cash is returned: capex, buybacks and dividends.

## Canadian footprint factors

| Factor | Why it matters | Where to check |
|:--|:--|:--|
| CAD/USD on imported goods | Many goods are bought in USD and sold in CAD | MD&A gross margin discussion; [currency guide](/investing/analyze/handle-currency-cad-vs-usd-results/) |
| Weather and seasonality | Winter and holiday quarters dominate some categories | Quarterly results over two years |
| Provincial regulation | Alcohol, pharmacy, cannabis and store hours vary by province | AIF regulatory section; [regulatory risk](/investing/analyze/analyze-regulatory-risk-disclosures-aif/) |
| Concentrated grocery and pharmacy market | Price competition and regulatory scrutiny | Industry discussion in the AIF |
| Geography mix | Some Canadian-listed brands earn most revenue abroad | [Geographic revenue mix](/investing/analyze/analyze-geographic-revenue-mix-canadian-multinationals/) |
| Financial services arms | Store credit cards add credit risk | Segment note; [segment reporting](/investing/analyze/read-segment-reporting-canadian-filings/) |

## Metrics to log every quarter

| Metric | What it tells you | Caution |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Same-store sales (traffic and average ticket) | Demand at existing stores | Definitions vary; e-commerce may be included |
| Gross margin | Pricing power, mix, shrink | Distribution costs may sit above or below gross profit |
| Inventory growth vs sales growth | Risk of markdowns | See [inventory turns](/investing/analyze/analyze-inventory-turns-receivables-quality/) |
| SG&A as % of sales | Wage and rent pressure | [Operating leverage](/investing/analyze/analyze-operating-leverage/) cuts both ways |
| Store count and new-store productivity | Growth runway | Cannibalization of nearby stores |
| Lease liabilities and lease-adjusted leverage | Fixed commitments | IFRS 16 puts leases on the balance sheet |
| Loyalty liability | Points owed to customers | Deferred revenue under IFRS 15 |

## Checklist

| Step | Done when |
|:--|:--|
| Geography mix noted | Share of revenue by country |
| Same-store sales split | Traffic vs ticket over four quarters |
| Margin drivers identified | Mix, shrink, FX and freight |
| Inventory checked | Growth vs sales growth |
| Leases and financial arms read | Lease-adjusted leverage; credit book quality |
| Cash return policy logged | Capex, buybacks and dividend trend |
