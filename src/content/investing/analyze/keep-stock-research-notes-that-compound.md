Most investors' research notes decay. Each company gets a fresh document, last year's insights get buried and the same lessons get relearned. Notes **compound** when they're structured so each new company and each mistake makes the next analysis faster and better. This post sets out a durable system: what to keep, where to keep it and how to review it.

Educational only, not financial advice. It ties together workflows that already exist on the site: [separating price from fundamentals](/investing/analyze/separate-price-action-from-fundamentals/), [the QoQ thesis log](/investing/analyze/track-stock-thesis-over-quarters/) and [the watchlist process](/investing/analyze/build-watchlist-process-not-noise/). It doesn't add another card.

## Direct answer

Keep three note types. A **company file** for each stock (a one-page summary at the top, then dated, append-only entries). **Concept notes**, one idea per note (for example, how to read covenant language), linked to the companies where you used them. And a **lessons log** of decisions and outcomes. Review company files quarterly after earnings and the whole system once a year: refresh each one-page summary, promote recurring insights into concept notes and record whether outcomes came from process or luck.

## Why notes fail and the fix

| Failure | Symptom | Fix |
|:--|:--|:--|
| No dates | Can't tell what you knew when | Date every entry; append, never overwrite |
| One giant document | Can't find anything after year one | One folder per company + separate concept notes |
| Price mixed with evidence | Notes read like a price diary | [Three-column layout](/investing/analyze/separate-price-action-from-fundamentals/) |
| No summary | Re-reading everything to remember the thesis | One-page summary at the top, refreshed quarterly |
| No lessons loop | Same mistakes repeated | Lessons log with "process or luck?" field |
| Tool hopping | Notes scattered across apps | Plain text or markdown, in a format you'll still be able to open in ten years |

## Suggested folder structure

| Folder | Contents | Example file names |
|:--|:--|:--|
| /companies/TICKER/ | Summary, thesis card, QoQ scorecard, dated entries | `summary.md`, `2026-Q3-debrief.md` |
| /concepts/ | One idea per note, linked to companies | `covenants.md`, `nim.md`, `dso.md` |
| /templates/ | Pre-earnings card, debrief scorecard, peer table | `pre-earnings.md`, `debrief.md` |
| /lessons/ | Decision journal, annual review | `2026-annual-review.md` |
| /sources/ | Filing excerpts with links and dates | `TICKER-2026-AIF-risk.md` |

Templates come from the [pre-earnings checklist](/investing/analyze/build-pre-earnings-checklist/), the [60-minute debrief](/investing/analyze/debrief-earnings-print-under-an-hour/) and the [stock brief template](/investing/analyze/stock-brief-template-bull-bear-watch/).

## Review cadence

| Cadence | Task | Time (rough) |
|:--|:--|--:|
| Per release | Debrief entry + scorecard | 60 min |
| Quarterly | Refresh the one-page summary per holding | 15 min each |
| Annually | Promote recurring insights to concept notes; lessons review | 2–4 hours |
| Annually | Diff AIF risk factors vs last year | 20 min each ([regulatory risk post](/investing/analyze/analyze-regulatory-risk-disclosures-aif/)) |
