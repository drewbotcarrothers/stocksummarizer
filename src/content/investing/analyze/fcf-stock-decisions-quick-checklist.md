You do not need another full essay on free cash flow. StockSummarizer already has an FCF analysis hub, a glossary definition, a P/FCF valuation spoke, and an FCF dividend-coverage post. This page is a **thinner decision checklist**: the gates to walk before FCF changes a buy, hold, trim, or kill call.

Educational only — not financial advice. Any numbers you plug into the checklist should be labelled **hypothetical** or sourced from filings. Re-angled from the bank title "How to Analyze Free Cash Flow for Stock Decisions" to avoid duplicating [Free cash flow analysis](/investing/analyze/free-cash-flow-analysis/).

## Direct answer

Before FCF moves your decision: (1) lock a definition and stick to it; (2) normalize for cycle, working capital, and one-times; (3) test competing claims on that cash (dividends, debt service, buybacks, growth capex); (4) map the result to thesis support, watch, or kill. For mechanics, open the hub posts linked below — do not rebuild them here.

## Decision checklist (print / copy)

| Gate | Pass looks like | Fail / watch looks like | Deep-dive link |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Definition locked | Same FCF formula YoY | Formula hops with the story | [FCF analysis hub](/investing/analyze/free-cash-flow-analysis/), [What is FCF?](/investing/glossary/what-is-free-cash-flow/) |
| Capex split clear | Maintenance vs growth stated or estimated | "All capex is growth" hand-waving | [Capex in the MD&A](/investing/analyze/read-capex-plans-mdna/) |
| Cycle / WC normalized | Mid-cycle sketch exists | Peak CFO treated as run-rate | [CFO vs NI](/investing/analyze/read-cfo-vs-net-income/), [WC swings](/investing/analyze/analyze-working-capital-swings-quarterly/) |
| Dividend claim | Coverage with margin of safety | Yield defended while FCF shrinks | [FCF coverage of dividends](/investing/dividends/fcf-coverage-of-dividends/) |
| Valuation context | P/FCF used with peers + cycle | Single-year trough FCF → "cheap" | [What P/FCF means](/investing/valuation/what-p-fcf-means-canadian-diy/) |
| Thesis mapping | FCF supports or challenges a written thesis | FCF used as a vibe check only | [Bull / Bear / Watch](/investing/analyze/stock-brief-template-bull-bear-watch/), [Thesis card](/investing/frameworks/thesis-catalyst-kill-criteria-card/) |

## Hypothetical mini-example (literacy only)

| Item | Hyp. figure | Checklist note |
|:--|:--|:--|
| CFO | $120M | From cash-flow statement |
| Maintenance capex (est.) | $40M | Your estimate — disclose it |
| Growth capex | $50M | Optional to exclude for "owner" FCF |
| FCF (CFO − all capex) | $30M | Stricter definition |
| Dividends | $28M | Coverage thin on strict FCF |
| Decision lean | Watch | Do not celebrate the dividend until coverage breathes |
