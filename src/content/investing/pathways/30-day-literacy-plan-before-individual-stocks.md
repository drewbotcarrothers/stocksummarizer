Most people buy their first individual stock after reading one article or watching one video. This plan asks for **30 days and about 18 hours** first. It turns the four-week sprint in our [Start Here pathway](/investing/pathways/canadian-diy-stock-investing-start-here/) into a **day-by-day schedule**, with one reading and one small written output per day, and ends with a readiness gate you either pass or don't.

Educational only — not financial advice, and no stock picks. Stay on the plan even if a "can't-miss" idea shows up on day 6. It will still exist on day 31.

## Direct answer

Spend week 1 on where the money sits and how much of it can go to individual stocks; week 2 reading one TSX company's real filings; week 3 learning the core metrics and building a valuation **range**; week 4 writing a one-page brief, sell criteria and a paper position. On days 29–30, run the readiness checklist below. If you can't tick every box, wait — keep the ETF core and repeat weeks 2–4 on a second company.

## The readiness gate (days 29–30)

| Check | Pass if… |
|:--|:--|
| Purpose | You can say in one sentence why this stock belongs in the satellite |
| Size | The position fits your written maximum and satellite budget |
| Account | You know which account it goes in and why |
| Business | You can explain where the profit comes from by segment |
| Numbers | You have five years of revenue, FCF per share, share count and debt |
| Valuation | Your range is written down, and today's price sits inside or below it |
| Risks | Three kill criteria are written down |
| Monitoring | Alerts are set and you have a weekly 15-minute slot |
| Costs | You know the commission, FX cost and spread you'll pay |

Fail any row? That's a useful result. Repeat weeks 2–4 on a second company, or stay with the ETF core — see [Canadian equity ETFs vs individual stocks](/investing/indexes-etfs/canadian-equity-etfs-vs-individual-stocks/).

## Common mistakes this plan avoids

| Mistake | Plan step that prevents it |
|:--|:--|
| Buying first, researching later | Days 8–21 come before any position |
| One giant position | Day 5 maximum size |
| Doubling Canada's bank and energy weight by accident | Day 6 concentration map |
| Anchoring to a price target | Day 21 valuation range |
| No exit plan | Day 24 kill criteria |
| Forgetting about the holding | Day 25 monitoring setup |
