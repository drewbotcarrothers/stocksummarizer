Stock research drowns in sources: filings, press releases, call transcripts, analyst notes, apps, videos, forum posts. When they disagree, you need a rule for which one wins. This card is that rule: **filings > transcripts > commentary**.

Educational framework — not financial advice. Scores in the chart are **illustrative**. This one-card summary links our deeper guides: [using SEDAR+ for stock research](/investing/analyze/use-sedar-plus-for-stock-research/), [reading an earnings call transcript](/investing/analyze/how-to-read-earnings-call-transcript/) and [evaluating third-party stock score apps](/investing/brokers-tools/evaluate-stock-score-apps-critically/).

## Why the order works

| Tier | Why it ranks here | Watch out for |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Audited annual filings** | Financial statements are audited; CEOs and CFOs certify annual and interim filings; continuous disclosure rules set content and deadlines | Lag — they describe the past |
| **Interim filings & material changes** | Same rulebook, reviewed rather than audited; material change reports are required for significant events | Quarterly noise |
| **News releases** | Timely and official, but written to frame the story; non-GAAP measures must be reconciled to the nearest IFRS figure | "Adjusted" headlines — see [read non-GAAP metrics critically](/investing/analyze/read-non-gaap-metrics-critically/) |
| **Calls & presentations** | Useful for tone, priorities and commitments; transcripts usually aren't filed documents | Forward-looking language, selective emphasis |
| **Analyst & data vendors** | Helpful synthesis and consensus, but derived from tiers 1–4 | Stale data, inconsistent definitions, conflicts |
| **Commentary** | Fast and sometimes insightful | No accountability; often recycled or wrong |

## How to use it

| Situation | Rule |
|:--|:--|
| An app's figure disagrees with the filing | Use the filing; find out how the app defines the metric |
| Management's call comment disagrees with the MD&A | Trust the MD&A; log the gap in your [commitment log](/investing/analyze/how-to-read-earnings-call-transcript/) |
| A headline claims something big happened | Look for a news release or material change report on [SEDAR+](/investing/analyze/sedar-plus-alerts-filing-types-monitoring/) |
| A kill criterion depends on a number | Source it from tier 1 or 2 only — see the [thesis → catalyst → kill criteria card](/investing/frameworks/thesis-catalyst-kill-criteria-card/) |
| You're short on time | Read tier 1–3 for one company before tier 5–6 on ten |

Lower tiers aren't useless — commentary can tell you *what questions to ask*. They just can't be the *answer*. That's also how StockSummarizer briefs are built: every brief lists its sources so you can climb back up the ladder — see [how to use briefs as learning objects](/investing/pathways/stocksummarizer-briefs-as-learning-objects/).
