A 7% or 8% yield looks like a paycheque. It can also be the market's way of saying the dividend is in trouble.

Educational myth-buster — not financial advice. Numbers are **hypothetical**. The full screening method is in [why yield alone is a terrible stock screen](/investing/dividends/why-yield-alone-is-a-terrible-screen/); this is the short version.

## The myth

> "It yields 8% and has paid a dividend for decades. That's safe income."

## The reality in one line

Dividend yield is **annual dividend ÷ share price**. When the price falls and the dividend hasn't been cut *yet*, the yield rises. A very high yield is often a warning, not a reward — safety comes from **coverage, balance sheet and earnings direction**, not from the yield number.

## Why a high yield can be unsafe

| What the yield hides | Why it matters | Where to look |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Coverage** | A dividend paid from more than free cash flow is borrowed time | [FCF coverage of dividends](/investing/dividends/fcf-coverage-of-dividends/) |
| **Payout ratio trend** | Rising toward or above 100% leaves no cushion | [Payout ratio explained](/investing/dividends/payout-ratio-explained-canadian-companies/) |
| **Debt load** | Lenders get paid before shareholders | MD&A liquidity section; [how to read an MD&A](/investing/analyze/how-to-read-mdna/) |
| **Earnings direction** | Falling earnings make today's payout tomorrow's cut | Last 4–8 quarters of results |
| **Structure** | REIT and trust distributions use different metrics | [REIT distributions vs corporate dividends](/investing/dividends/reit-distributions-vs-corporate-dividends/) |

## A track record is not a guarantee

A long history of payments shows a board's *intention*. It doesn't stop a cut when cash runs short. Even dividend-growth indexes are built on dividend **history** rules, not on coverage or balance-sheet tests — see [Canadian Dividend Aristocrats explained](/investing/dividends/canadian-dividend-aristocrats-explained/).

## Safer framing

| Instead of asking… | Ask… |
|:--|:--|
| "What's the highest yield I can get?" | "Which dividends are well covered and growing?" |
| "Has it ever been cut?" | "What would force a cut, and how close is that?" |
| "Is 8% better than 4%?" | "What total return do I expect — yield **plus** growth, minus risk?" |

If you want income with less single-stock risk, compare [dividend growth vs high yield frameworks](/investing/dividends/dividend-growth-vs-high-yield/) and keep any one payer within your [position-size limit](/investing/process/position-sizing-diy-stock-pickers/).
