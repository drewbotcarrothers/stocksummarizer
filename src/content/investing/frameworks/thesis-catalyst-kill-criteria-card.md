Most investors can say why they bought a stock. Few wrote down, *before* buying, what would prove them wrong. This card makes you do both in one place: **thesis → catalyst → kill criteria**, plus the size and valuation limits that keep a good idea from becoming a big mistake.

Educational framework — not financial advice. Examples are **hypothetical**. This is the printable one-card version of [documenting sell criteria before you buy](/investing/process/document-sell-criteria-before-you-buy/), which covers the types of sell rules and the behavioural research in depth.

## Each box in one line

| Box | Answers | Hypothetical example |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Thesis** | Why will this be worth more than today's price? | "Fee-based cash flows fund a growing dividend; expansion adds cash flow in two years." |
| **Catalysts** | What should happen, and by when? | "Expansion in service by end of next year." |
| **Time stop** | When do I re-underwrite if the catalyst doesn't arrive? | "If it slips 12 months, start from zero." |
| **Kill criteria** | What observable fact proves the thesis wrong? | "Payout above 90% of distributable cash flow for four quarters." |
| **Limits** | How big can it get, and at what price is upside used up? | "Max 8% of portfolio; trim above my high-case value." |

Two or three kill criteria are enough. If you need six, the thesis is fuzzy. A falling price belongs in **review triggers**, not kill criteria.

| What you find | What to do |
|:--|:--|
| No kill criterion hit, catalyst on track | Hold; note the date of the review |
| Catalyst late but before the time stop | Hold; tighten the watch |
| Time stop passed | Re-underwrite from scratch as if you didn't own it |
| Kill criterion hit | Exit or reduce — unless you can write down *why the criterion was wrong* |
| Position above max size | Trim back to the limit |

## Where the inputs come from

Kill criteria only work if they're checkable. Tie each one to a document: the quarterly report and MD&A ([how to read an MD&A](/investing/analyze/how-to-read-mdna/)), material change reports via [SEDAR+ alerts](/investing/analyze/sedar-plus-alerts-filing-types-monitoring/), and management's stated targets on the [earnings call](/investing/analyze/how-to-read-earnings-call-transcript/). Our [source hierarchy card](/investing/frameworks/source-hierarchy-filings-transcripts-commentary/) ranks which source wins when they disagree.
