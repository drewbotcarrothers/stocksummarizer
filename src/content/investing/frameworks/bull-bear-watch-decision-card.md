A full stock brief is a page. A **decision card** is the part you actually look at when you're about to click "buy": three bulls, three bears, the dated things to watch, and a decision box you tick and date.

Educational framework — not financial advice, no recommendations. Scores and examples are **hypothetical**. This card compresses our [stock brief template (bull / bear / watch)](/investing/analyze/stock-brief-template-bull-bear-watch/) into one printable view; use the template for the research and this card for the decision.

## How to fill it in five minutes

| Box | Rule | Bad example | Good example (hypothetical) |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **Bull** | What must be true from *today's* price | "Great company" | "Loan growth 4%+ with credit losses near the 5-year average" |
| **Bear** | What's priced in or overlooked — be as specific as the bull | "Market could fall" | "Office loan book reprices higher on refinancing" |
| **Watch** | Observable KPI or catalyst **with a date** | "Earnings" | "Q4 provisions in the December report" |
| **Decision** | Tick one box, set a max size, sign and date | Undecided | "Wait — revisit after Q4" |

If a box is hard to fill, that's the finding. An empty bear box means you haven't done the work yet.

| Bull evidence | Bear severity | Default action |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Strong (7–10) | Mild (0–4) | **Act** with a starter position inside your [size limit](/investing/process/position-sizing-diy-stock-pickers/) |
| Strong | Moderate (5–6) | **Wait** for one Watch item to resolve |
| Weak or mixed | Mild or moderate | **Wait** — keep on the watchlist |
| Any | Severe (7–10) | **Pass** unless the bear case is clearly priced in |

"Act" is never "buy everything now." It's a starter position with exit rules written first — pair this card with the [thesis → catalyst → kill criteria card](/investing/frameworks/thesis-catalyst-kill-criteria-card/).

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|:--|:--|
| Bulls are facts, bears are vague | Write both sides at the same level of detail |
| Watch items with no dates | Every Watch line needs a "by when" |
| Re-scoring after the price moves | Re-score only after new information (results, filings) |
| No "Pass" ever ticked | A good process passes on most ideas |
