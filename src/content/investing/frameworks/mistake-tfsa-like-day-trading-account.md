A TFSA's tax-free growth makes it tempting to trade aggressively inside it: no capital-gains tax on winners, so why not trade more? Because if the CRA decides your TFSA is **carrying on a business**, that tax-free status can disappear on the gains.

Educational only — not tax or legal advice. Examples are **hypothetical**. The rules, the CRA factors and case law are in [risk of business-like trading inside a TFSA](/investing/accounts/tfsa-business-like-trading-risk/); this is the short symptom → cost → fix version.

## Symptom

You use the TFSA as your most active account — frequent in-and-out trades, short holding periods, a lot of time spent watching screens, and positions chosen for quick moves rather than a business thesis.

## Cost

A TFSA trust is taxable on income from carrying on a business. Whether trading counts as a business is a **question of fact** — there's no safe number of trades. If the CRA concludes it is, gains can be reassessed as fully taxable business income, and the holder is **jointly and severally liable** with the trust for that tax. In 2024 the Federal Court of Appeal agreed that a TFSA trading qualified investments can still be carrying on a taxable business.

| What you expected | What a reassessment can mean (hypothetical) |
|:--|:--|
| $40,000 of gains, tax-free | $40,000 taxed as business income |
| No paperwork | Reassessment, interest, possible dispute |
| Room keeps compounding | Tax paid from outside, or from the account |

## Fix

| Habit | Lower-risk version |
|:--|:--|
| Trade on news or charts | Buy with a written thesis — use the [thesis → catalyst → kill criteria card](/investing/frameworks/thesis-catalyst-kill-criteria-card/) |
| Hold days or weeks | Hold for the length of the thesis, typically years |
| High turnover | Rebalance a few times a year at most |
| Speculative small caps for quick pops | Keep speculation small and, if you must, in a taxable account |
| Everything in one account | Run the TFSA as an investment account inside a [core-satellite plan](/investing/accounts/core-satellite-canadian-accounts/) |

Frequent trading also tends to hurt returns through costs and behaviour — see [behavioural biases that hurt stock pickers](/investing/process/behavioural-biases-stock-pickers/) and the hidden costs in [commission-free trading: FX and spreads](/investing/brokers-tools/commission-free-trading-fx-spreads/).
