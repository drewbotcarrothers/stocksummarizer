Seven liquid names — Teck, First Quantum, Lundin Mining, Hudbay, Ivanhoe Mines, Capstone Copper and Ero Copper — are about **CA$133 billion** of market value on the October 23, 2026 close. Over the past month the peer median is about **−18.6%**, against an S&P/TSX Composite that was about **+0.1%** on the same exchange-close window. Over one year the median is about **+39%**, well ahead of a Composite near **+18.6%**. COMEX copper futures settled near **US$6.48** a pound on October 23, down about **3%** from about **US$6.68** on September 23, and up about **27%** from about **US$5.08** on October 23, 2025. The stocks fell much harder than the metal over the month, and they beat the metal over the year. Setup for this living note: a one-year copper re-rating, an October air pocket, and two multiples that are not a normal producer P/E.

## What is driving Canadian base-metals stocks: a copper year, then a harder October

1. **The metal and the stocks parted company in October.** From September 23 to October 23, COMEX copper futures fell about **3%**, from about **US$6.68** to about **US$6.48** a pound. The peer-median stock fell about **19%**. From October 16 to October 23 the futures move was smaller still, about **US$6.53** to **US$6.48**. This table’s one-month drop is an equity de-rating on top of a small move in the screen price of copper. Over one year the futures are up about **27%** and the median stock is up about **39%**. The year was the re-rating. October gave a piece of the equity re-rating back.

2. **October 28–29, 2026 — First Quantum, Teck and Capstone, on the calendar.** TradingView’s earnings calendar, checked October 27, shows October 28 for First Quantum and October 29 for Teck and Capstone. Those are calendar dates, not a confirmation of the call time. First Quantum is the name with the trailing loss and an **83.5x** forward multiple. Teck is the largest market cap and the only Neutral rating in the set, with **+6.3%** to the CAD target. Capstone is the pure copper producer at **13.1x** trailing earnings. The three prints are the nearest fact. This note does not have a separate October operating release for any of them.

3. **November 2–12 — Ero, Ivanhoe, Lundin Mining and Hudbay.** The same calendar shows November 2 for Ero Copper, November 3 for Ivanhoe, November 4 for Lundin Mining and November 12 for Hudbay. Ero is the one-year leader and the only name in the set up over three months and six months. Ivanhoe is the one-year laggard, with **CA$810 million** of trailing revenue against a **CA$13.1 billion** market cap. Lundin Mining and Hudbay are the other two one-year winners above **40%**.

4. **First Quantum’s earnings are a loss, and the forward multiple is the tell.** Trailing net income is **−CA$112 million**. There is no trailing P/E. The forward P/E of **83.5x** is October 23’s price divided by a next-year EPS estimate that is still small. Free cash flow screens at **294x**, which means cash flow is a sliver of the market cap. There is no dividend. The CAD target is **CA$46.78** from **12** analysts, **+40%** above **$33.44**. A twelve-analyst gap on a loss-making copper producer is a recovery target. It is not evidence that 83x is a normal multiple.

5. **Ivanhoe’s price is a claim about future sales.** Trailing revenue is **CA$810 million** and trailing profit is **CA$191 million**, so the trailing P/E is **67.8x** and the forward P/E is **41.3x**. Price to sales is **16.2x**, in royalty territory, on a miner. The one-year return is **−37.1%**. The CAD target is **CA$14.72** from **10** analysts, **+61%** above **$9.16**. The Street’s gap and the one-year tape point in opposite directions. This page does not add a production forecast on top of those figures.

6. **What this note leaves out on purpose.** Cameco (about **CA$48 billion**) is uranium, files in the same broad minerals sector, and has its own brief. Nutrien is fertilizer. Centerra (about **CA$5.5 billion**) is filed under precious metals and is left off this page and the gold page, so a gold-primary name is not dropped into a copper table because the starting list mentioned it. Teck’s Class A line is the illiquid class; the table is the Class B. Gold, silver and the streamers are on the gold page. They are not repeated here.

## Which Canadian base-metals stocks are leading and lagging

**One month:** nobody is green. **Teck (−12.2%)** fell the least, then **Lundin Mining (−15.4%)** and **Ero (−18.3%)**. **First Quantum (−26.0%)** is the laggard, then **Ivanhoe (−24.9%)** and **Capstone (−23.2%)**. The three smallest copper-pure names and the loss-making major took the harder hit. Teck, the diversified large cap, was the relative hold.

**One year:** **Ero (+46.2%)** leads, then **Lundin Mining (+44.1%)**, **Hudbay (+42.7%)** and **Teck (+39.2%)**. **Ivanhoe (−37.1%)** is the hole in the middle of the set. **Capstone (−1.6%)** is the other name under zero. First Quantum’s **+11.9%** one-year price return is a gain on a stock that still has a trailing loss. The median of **+39%** is those four winners. It is not Ivanhoe, and it is not the whole complex moving together.

## How Teck and Ivanhoe differ from the copper producers

**Teck** is the diversified large cap: the most revenue, a Neutral rating, a small dividend, and the smallest upside in the set. The October drop was real and it was also the shallowest. **Lundin Mining, Hudbay, Capstone and Ero** are the operating copper and zinc names where trailing earnings exist and the forward multiple is in a range a producer comparison can use. Ero is the one that already ran. Capstone is the one that did not, over one year, and still shows a **56%** CAD gap.

**First Quantum** is a large copper business with a trailing loss and a free-cash-flow multiple that should not be averaged with Ero’s **20x**. **Ivanhoe** is a large market cap on a small income statement. Both have double-digit analyst counts and large CAD gaps. Both are a different bet from Teck at **13x** forward earnings.

## Bull case for Canadian base-metals stocks

1. **The one-year winners are still winners, and copper is up over the year.** Ero, Lundin Mining, Hudbay and Teck are up about **39% to 46%**. COMEX copper is up about **27%** over the same window. The October equity drop of about **19%** at the median happened against a copper move of about **3%**.

2. **The clean multiples are not heroic.** Ero at **7.5x** forward, Teck at **13.1x**, Capstone at **13.2x** and Hudbay at **14.4x** are producer math. Lundin Mining’s trailing **12.1x** is in that neighbourhood even though the forward figure is **15.8x**.

3. **The CAD targets have analyst counts.** Every row in the target table has at least eight analysts, and five of the seven still show **35%** or more to the average. That is a different shape from the gold royalties, where the gap on Franco-Nevada is **12%**, and from the Canadian banks, where several names sit on the target.

## Bear case and risks for Canadian base-metals stocks

1. **October was an equity event.** A **3%** dip in the copper screen price and a **19%** dip in the median stock means the stocks can fall without a copper crash. Another month like that does not need a new low in the metal.

2. **Two multiples will wreck an average.** First Quantum at **83.5x** forward and **294x** free cash flow, and Ivanhoe at **67.8x** trailing and **16.2x** sales, are in the table so the loss and the thin revenue are visible. They are not the sector multiple. The snapshot uses the median for that reason. The ten-year average is Capstone’s **+1,392%**.

3. **Teck and Ero do not have much Canadian-dollar room.** **+6.3%** and **+15.8%** are what is left after the year they had. A target that sat still while the stock fell is not the same thing as a target on a stock that has already arrived. The November calendar will show which one this is.

## What to watch next for Canadian base-metals stocks: October 28, then Ivanhoe’s week

1. **October 28, 2026 — First Quantum, on TradingView’s calendar.** The loss-making major. The question is whether the next-year earnings estimate behind the **83.5x** forward multiple moves, and whether the stock is still the month’s worst name after the print.

2. **October 29 — Teck and Capstone.** The Neutral large cap with **+6%** of gap, and the copper producer at **13x** with **+56%** of gap. Same morning on the calendar, opposite setups.

3. **November 2 — Ero. November 3 — Ivanhoe. November 4 — Lundin Mining. November 12 — Hudbay.** Ero is the leader the Street has mostly priced. Ivanhoe is the revenue gap. Lundin Mining is the one-year winner whose three-month return is **−18.6%**.

4. **COMEX copper against the US$6.48 October 23 settlement.** The stocks overshot the metal on the way down in October. The next useful check is whether they overshoot it on the way back, or whether the de-rating sticks through the results.

5. **Target refreshes after those prints.** A **+36%** average upside that survives the November numbers is a different fact from a **+36%** average that was set before a **19%** month.
