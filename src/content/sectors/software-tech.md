Nine liquid names — Shopify, Thomson Reuters, Constellation Software, Celestica, CGI, Descartes, OpenText, Topicus and Kinaxis — are about **CA$554 billion** of market value on the October 25, 2026 screener. Shopify alone is about **CA$304 billion**. Over the past month the peer median is about **+18%**, far ahead of the S&P/TSX Composite’s approximate **+1.4%**. Over one year the median is about **−8%**, against a Composite near **+18%**. Celestica is the exception inside the month: it is down about **29%** over one month, and its October 26 earnings landed after the close in this table. Setup for this living note: a rebound that is real, a one-year hole that is also real, and a target column that has to stay in Canadian dollars.

## What is driving Canadian software and tech stocks: AI headlines, a hardware print and a November stack

1. **October 21, 2026 — Constellation names a Chief AI Officer.** Daan Dijkhuizen, who also runs the Topicus operating group and VMS Ventures, became Chief AI Officer. The October 26 brief is the write-up. Constellation’s third-quarter release is **November 6**, with the call on **November 9 at 8:00 a.m. ET**. Second-quarter figures in that brief, in US dollars: revenue **US$3.34 billion**, up 17%, with organic growth about 3% (about 1% after the company’s FX adjustment). The one-month tape in the October 25 file is **+22.1%**. The one-year tape is still **−9.2%**.

2. **October 22, 2026 — Thomson Reuters launches CoCounsel for tax and audit.** The stock’s launch-day move was about **+6.5%** on the TSX. The October 25 brief is the write-up. Third-quarter results are **November 3**. The one-month screener return is **+28.0%**, the best in this set. The one-year return is still **−24.6%**. The guide discussed in that brief is roughly 8% organic growth, with the Big 3 segments nearer 9.5–10%.

3. **October 26, 2026 — Celestica’s quarter, after this table’s close.** Celestica reported third-quarter revenue of **US$5.83 billion**, up 83% from **US$3.19 billion**, GAAP EPS of **US$3.26** (US$2.31 a year earlier) and adjusted EPS of **US$3.30** (US$1.58). Adjusted operating margin was **8.5%**. The 2026 outlook went to revenue of **US$21.0 billion** and adjusted EPS of **US$11.55**. The first 2027 outlook is revenue of **US$40.0 billion**, adjusted EPS of **US$21.50** and an adjusted operating margin of about **9%**. Free-cash-flow guidance for 2026 was cut to **US$460 million** from **US$600 million**, which the company tied to working-capital investment for 2027. The quarter also included the previously announced share offering for aggregate gross proceeds of **US$3.45 billion**. Management intends to file a new normal-course issuer bid after the current one expires on **November 2, 2026**, for up to **2.5%** of the public float, if the TSX accepts it. The call and investor day were **October 27 at 8:00 a.m. ET**. All of those figures are US dollars. The **$364.40** in the table is the October 23 TSX close, before this print. The October 26 close was lower. The one-month drop of about **29%** was already in the price before the release.

4. **November 3 — Shopify, the same morning as Thomson Reuters.** Shopify said on October 6 that it will report the third quarter (period ended September 30) before the open on Tuesday, November 3, with a call at **8:30 a.m. ET**. Shopify is more than half of this peer set’s market cap and it pays no dividend. The September 25 brief is the Meta AI-checkout note, on an older screener.

5. **November 5 — OpenText. November 11 — CGI.** OpenText said on October 8 that it will report first-quarter fiscal 2027 before the open on November 5, with a call at **8:00 a.m. ET** (CEO Ayman Antoun, CFO Steve Rai). **One year:** **Kinaxis (+13.3%)** is the leader, and **Shopify (+1.2%)** is the only other name above zero. **Topicus (−32.6%)** is the laggard, then **OpenText (−28.5%)** and **Thomson Reuters (−24.6%)**. ## How the compounders differ from Shopify, Celestica and the spin-off

**Constellation** is the acquisition compounder: a token dividend, a forward multiple far below the trailing multiple, and a one-year drawdown that October only partly repaired. **Topicus** is the European spin-off on the Venture exchange. It is not a second copy of the parent. Trailing profit is **CA$58 million**, the one-year return is the worst in the set, and there is no ten-year history. **CGI** is the IT-services operator that reports in Canadian dollars and screens at a market multiple. **OpenText** is the cheap, high-yield (for this group) enterprise-software name that has not participated in the one-year recovery. **Descartes** and **Kinaxis** are the smaller software compounders, both without a dividend, Kinaxis the one-year price leader.

**Thomson Reuters** is information services with a legal and tax franchise, not a vertical-market software roll-up. The CoCounsel launch is why it led the month. The CAD target under the price is why “led the month” is not the same sentence as “cheap.”

**Shopify** is the platform. It is half the market cap of this note and it does not belong in a dividend average. **Celestica** is the factory. Its five-year chart is a re-rating (**+3,060%**). Its one-month chart is a giveback (**−28.7%**), and the October 26 outlook raised revenue while cutting the free-cash-flow guide.

## Bull case for Canadian software and tech stocks

1. **The one-month rebound is broad.** Eight of the nine names are up over the month, and the median is about **+18%** against a TSX near **+1%**. Constellation’s **+22%** month and Thomson Reuters’ **+28%** month are in the October 25 file, not a rounding choice.

2. **Constellation still has a CAD gap, and OpenText and CGI are not priced like Shopify.** **+15.8%** to **CA$3,931** is a ten-analyst Canadian target. OpenText at **7.1x** forward and CGI at **12.8x** forward are the valves if a buyer does not want **88x** forward at Shopify or **54x** trailing at Constellation.

3. **Celestica’s 2027 outlook is a number, not a slogan.** **US$40 billion** of revenue and **US$21.50** of adjusted EPS are management’s initial 2027 outlook, in US dollars, from the October 26 release. The stock’s October 23 price does not include the reaction to that release. The reaction, through October 26, was down, not up. That is a fact about the tape, not a forecast.

## Bear case and risks for Canadian software and tech stocks

1. **The one-year hole is still there.** Median about **−8%**, with Topicus, OpenText and Thomson Reuters down more than **24%**, against a TSX up about **18%**. A one-month bounce can be short covering and multiple repair. It is not a new base until the one-year column turns.

2. **The forward P/E on this page is a method, and the file’s own forward P/E was lower for the compounders.** Constellation’s file print was **18.95**, not the **20.6** in this table. Thomson Reuters was **26.37**, not **28.2**. Using the higher figure does not make the stocks cheaper. Shopify at **87.7x** on this method is a reminder that “forward” still means expensive when the estimate is not a step-change.

3. **US targets and thin CAD pages will mislead anyone who averages them.** Descartes has no TSE price target. Shopify’s four-analyst **CA$198** and OpenText’s two-analyst **CA$27.50** are not the 49-analyst and 13-analyst US pages. Celestica’s NYSE target is a different panel from the nine-analyst CAD target. Mix those and the upside is fiction.
